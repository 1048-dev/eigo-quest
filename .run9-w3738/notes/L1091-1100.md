#### L1091 If I Were a Dragon
- A: Saffron on the cushions, the riddle card and Kiko's "I could tell Saffron what I saw" are warm and natural. In seg17, "Change it to you." is not something a teacher would say. In seg19, "If I were means just imagine." is broken English.
- B: "Even with I, we say were!" is the right rule, and "If I were ~, I would + base verb" is modeled well. Same seg17 / seg19 flags as A. The pause rule (pause after was / then / sat / stood, never inside "a tiny dragon") is right.
- C: seg9 "Small and slow, then BIG!" may be read B-I-G. The pauses after "was …" and "sat …" are easy to perform.
- Decision: seg17 -> "Make it about you." (A+B). seg19 -> "If I were means we're just imagining." (A+B). seg9 "BIG" -> "big" (B+C).
