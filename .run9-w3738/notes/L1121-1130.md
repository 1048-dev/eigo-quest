#### Block-wide notes
- **Capital "UP" / "DOWN" / "ON" / "OVER" / "TURN" in say** for phrasal-verb stress (L1116-1119, L1125 seg10, L1130 seg11, and the W38 reviews). A TTS voice may spell "UP" as U-P. Consider lower case in `say` ("say up strongly") and keep the capitals in subtitles and slides, or check the TTS engine once. Pattern: `"say": "[^"]*\b(UP|DOWN|ON|OVER|OUT|TURN)\b`.
- **"Fru, s, tra, ted"** in an L1111-1114 say ("Frustrated. Fru, s, tra, ted. No extra e in the middle!") uses the same odd split I fixed in L1130. w38Phonics.ts says frus + trat + ed.
- **Unattributed quotes after narration** ("X frowns. But ...", "X stops. Let's ...") are common in W38 teacherScripts. Fix them where the quote could be heard as Ms. Emma's own words, not everywhere.
- **JA opening "みんな、やあ！"** (L1122, L1128, L1130) vs "やあ、みんな！" (block-wide). Both are OK. Unify if you want.
