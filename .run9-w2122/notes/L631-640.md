These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, "A. Or B.", OK, US praise, lowercase TPR after "Now,", L633/L638 「すぎた かたち」). There are 32 patches in 10 lessons, so every lesson has at least one change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. No test or src line references any changed text (I grepped tests/ and src/). The English in this world is clean, and the fixes are small. Themes: (1) **wrong or misleading phonics rules**: L631 tipJa「e を とって r だけ」gives "writr", and L632/L635 say that words ending in a t sound take -or, although writer and painter were taught one lesson earlier. (2) Grammar explanations that are not quite right: L638 "tried to = what a character did", and L638 seg8 "but he didn't" where the JA says できなかった. (3) Lines that read badly aloud: "What's it about, asks...", "Add, too!", "But shows a surprise!", "Because!", and the L631 "one thousand, one thousand..." count. (4) Small JA fixes: kanji 図書館 and 語の家族 in kid-facing text. Every new English sentence has 15 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The lessons are 205-224 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L631 A Library Made of Amber
- A: The arrival scene, the owl librarian and the sign are natural. The sign's "Please speak softly." is fine as written text. In the teacherScript, "One thousand, one thousand, one thousand, a thousand times!" does not explain a million. "Great asking!" (seg19) is not something a teacher says. In the AI starter, Kiko says "Welcome to the Amber Library!" as if she worked there, but she has only just found it.
- B: tipJa「write は e を とって r だけ つけるよ」is a wrong rule: if you drop the e and add only r, you get "writr". seg10 says it correctly ("ends with E, so just add R"). conversation.lines[2].ja has kanji 図書館, while seg7 says としょかん. The starter's role problem is the same as A's flag.
- C: The thousand count is a stumble and does not say what it means. "Great asking!" sounds odd. "Hoo, hoo!" and "Whisper, Wow." read well.
- Decision: teacherScript -> "It's a thousand times a thousand!" (A+B+C). tipJa -> 「e で おわって いるから、r だけ」 (objective JA). lines[2].ja 図書館 -> としょかん (objective JA). starter "Welcome to the Amber Library!" -> "Wow, look!" (A+B). seg19 "Great asking!" -> "Great question!" (A+C).

#### L632 What's It About?
- A: The New Books Corner and the Whiskers back-cover blurb are natural. "It's about plus a thing or a person." is fine. A US teacher would not teach "a t sound at the end often takes or": writer, painter, reporter and printer are all counterexamples.
- B: The same flag. The rule appears in seg9 and in the tipJa, but L631 has just taught writer and painter with -er. The teacherScript already gives the right advice ("O r sounds just like e r, so remember the spelling"). In seg8, "What's it about, asks what happens." puts a comma between the subject and the verb.
- C: seg8 reads like a list ("What's it about, asks, what happens") and is hard to phrase.
- Decision: tipJa and seg9 (say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa) -> "O R and E R sound the same, so remember the spelling." (A+B). seg8 -> "What's it about? That asks what happens. What kind of book? That asks the type." (B+C).

#### Block-wide notes
- **"t sound -> -or" rule** (L632 tipJa/seg9, L635 tipJa): writer and painter (L631) and writer (L635) contradict it. I replaced it with "same sound, remember the spelling". If other lessons in L641-660 or src/lib/curriculum/w22Phonics.ts explanations repeat 「t の おと」→ or, please check them.
- **「e を とって r だけ」** (L631 tipJa) was wrong. L634 tipJa「e を とってから er / or」is correct and kept. Check other "-e + er" tips for the same slip.
- **Kanji in kid-facing JA**: 図書館 (L631 lines[2].ja) and 語の家族 (L640 tipJa) were fixed. school.testPoints[1] in L640 and the tests still say 語の家族, which I kept because those are adult-facing.

- **"Ist!" (L633 seg9)**: please spot-check how the TTS reads "Ist!" (it should be the syllable /ɪst/).
