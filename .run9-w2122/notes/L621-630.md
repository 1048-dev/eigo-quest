### Run 9 review notes: L621-630 (world 21, Stage 3)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), and testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, "A. Or B.", OK, US praise, lowercase after "Now," / "Then,", L612 title, 「すぎた かたち」). There are 56 patches in 10 lessons, so every lesson has at least one change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. The English in this part of world 21 (emails, letters, online safety, opinions, the tower review and the radio show) is clean and natural, and the fixes are small. Themes: (1) **invented linking respellings in the "Sound time!" segments** (Holdon, Canai, Comin, Checki-tout, Thinka-bou-tit, Leeva, Aneemail, Signin, Turnon). TTS will misread them, and "Thinka-bou-tit" ends in a rude word when read aloud. I changed them to joined real words (Hold-on, Check-it-out) or to the hyphen beat form (Lee-va, Tur-non). (2) **Misquotes** where the teacher says "X says, ..." but the quote does not match the line or the learning item (L624 seg6 and the "home address" repeat-after-me in seg14, L629 seg7). (3) **"Ment makes a thing" / "Ness makes a feeling word"** are inaccurate (enjoyment and kindness are not things or feelings), so I changed them to "makes a noun". (4) Pacing words in narration in L629 ("last week", "month", "Tomorrow"). (5) One AI starter (L625) where the app partner Haru introduces himself as Leo. The longest new sentence has 9 words. The check script passes: ALL OK. The lessons are 205-222 s long with 100% English share, and every slide item is valid.

#### L621 Dear Ms. Emma
- A: The email in three parts (greeting, message, closing) and Kiko's snow email are natural. "While we were sleeping, it snowed a lot." is a nice past continuous preview. In seg20, "end kindly" is a little odd, but it is clear (single flag, kept). "From Kiko" as a kid's sign-off is fine.
- B: "Dear Ms. Emma, How are you?" with a capital H is fine as letter format (learning item). conversation.lines[2].ja says おしらせします, but the EN is "I want to tell you", and expressions[1].ja says おしらせしたいです.
- C: In seg9, "Holdon! Canai! Comin!" are invented spellings. TTS will read "Comin" as "coming" and "Canai" in an unpredictable way.
- Decision: seg9 -> "Hold-on! Can-I! Come-in!" (B+C). lines[2].ja -> おしらせしたいです (objective JA).

It is a vocab item and a title (titles.json), so I did not change it. Decide globally if you want "bird mail".
