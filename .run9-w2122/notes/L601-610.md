### Run 9 review notes: L601-610 (world 21, Stage 3)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, "A. Or B.", OK, US praise, lowercase after "Now," / "Then,", L612 title, bathroom, すぎた かたち). There are 38 patches in 8 lessons. L602 and L603 have no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. The English in this world is clean and natural (phone and video-call language is real US usage), so most fixes are about story logic and the AI partner. Themes: (1) **AI starters that speak as the wrong person**. The partner checker only looks for "I'm X", so it missed L605 ("It's me, Riko." but the partner is Kiko) and L606 ("This is Ms. Emma..." but the partner is Riko). L601 goalJa also told the child to ask エマ先生 although the partner is Haru. (2) Story logic: in L607, "We can only see your ears!" means the camera is on, so "Can you turn on your camera?" was not the logical quiz answer. In L609, the reading put Haru's bed on the left, but the conversation and quiz put it on the right. In L608, the wind was in the tower in the script but on the island in seg0. (3) Pacing words in teacher narration: "The weekend is coming", "See you on the weekend!" (L605) and "Great job this week!" (L610). (4) Small TTS and wording points: "Like, dislike" as the first pair (reads as filler "like"), "This is, is polite", "?," mid-sentence, "Draw a card", "big school", "tell your address". Every new sentence has 15 words or fewer. The check script passes with ALL OK. Touched lessons are 219-226 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.
