### Run 9 review notes: L651-660 (world 22, Stage 3)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, "A. Or B.", OK, US praise, lowercase TPR after "Now,", L652 bathroom, 「すぎた かたち」). There are 55 patches in 10 lessons, so every lesson has at least one change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. The only learning-item-like fields I touched are two intonation arrows: L651 phonics.sounds[2] (Who's there) and L652 phonics.sounds[0] (Where are you), both "↗" -> "↘". No test or src file checks w22 sounds (I grepped tests/ and src/lib/curriculum/). The English in this world is clean and the fixes are small. Themes: (1) **wh-question intonation was inconsistent**. L652, L654 and L655 teach "who/where questions go down", but L651 said "Who's there? Your voice goes up" and L652 had "↗" for Where are you even though its own tip and seg9 say down. (2) **Story facts and rules that do not match**: L652 "Kai said, Where are you?" right after "For questions, use asked"; L654 "Be the fox. Look out!" when the moon says it; L658 "three parts" vs "four parts"; L660 "a shy dragon. She's brave and kind." (Ember is he). (3) **Syllable spell-outs** ("strong gi, soft ant", "strong sec, soft ond", "soft cil", "soft a", "strong chil, soft dren") changed to the hyphen beat form "Gi-ant. Strong, then soft." in all five review lessons (L656-L660). (4) Small grammar/wording: "That's surprised", "What has pages, but it's not a tree?", "It's so adventurous!" for a scene, "His robot" with no antecedent. Every new English sentence has 15 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The lessons are 209-227 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L651 "Help!" Shouted the Boy
- A: The whale picture book and "Splash, splash!" opening are natural. In seg6, "At the beach, the boy says softly, Thank you, whispered the boy." tells us twice who spoke and how. seg7 "Read it soft." would be "Read it softly." ("Read it loud." is idiomatic, kept).
- B: The quotation-mark rule (line first, then who said it and how) is clear. seg8 says "Who's there? Your voice goes up.", but the next lessons teach that who/where questions go down, and phonics.sounds[2] is "↗". A knock-knock "Who's there?" falls. Same flag on seg6 (double attribution) and seg7 (adverb).
- C: seg6 is clumsy to read aloud: two speech verbs for one short line. seg9 "Now knock, knock. Who's there?" reads well.
- Decision: seg6 -> "At the beach, the boy is very quiet. Thank you, whispered the boy." and JA to match (A+B+C). seg7 "Read it soft." -> "Read it softly." (A+B). phonics.sounds[2] "↗" -> "↘", seg8 "goes up" -> "goes down", JA あがる -> さがる, slide.items[4] "↗" -> "↘" (objective, wrong rule).
