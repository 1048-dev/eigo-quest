These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, "A. Or B.", OK, US praise, lowercase TPR after "Now,", L644 "But that's all for today!" and its quiz distractor, quoted speech without quotation marks). There are 51 patches in 9 lessons. L646 has no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. The only learning-item field I touched is the Japanese of L643 expressions[2]. The Shy Dragon / Secret Door / Book That Won't Open storyline is clean and warm, and the fixes are small. Themes: (1) **TTS traps in the stress-beat spell-outs**: the capital beats "GI-ant" (L644, L645), "LI-on", "PI-lot" and "SEC-ond" (L642) look like acronyms and are likely read as letters, and "A-sleep" (L641) is likely read with the letter name, which is the exact sound the lesson warns against. (2) Invented sound respellings ("Don't say ay", "It sounds like ul", "Ul and us"). (3) Broken or telegraphic model lines ("No key, no open!", "Scared, so ran away. Ran away because scared."). (4) Grammar and rule errors in teacherScript ("Since that day, Ember was afraid", "When joins two things that happened at the same time"). (5) Pacing words in teacher narration ("Yesterday", "tomorrow" in L642). (6) Two AI starters that are hard to answer (L648 and L649, plus a double question in L642). Every new English sentence has 14 words or fewer (the longest is the L644 seg7 quote with "Professor Quill says,"). The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 206-224 s long with 100% English share in band, and every slide item is valid.
