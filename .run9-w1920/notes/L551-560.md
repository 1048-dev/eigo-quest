#### Block-wide notes
- **"And now, Smile!" / "And now, Blow the whistle!"**: a capital after "And now," before a plain TPR command, fixed here in L553, L554, L557 and L559 seg12. The block-wide lowercase pass covered "Now," but not "And now,". Scan L561-600 for `And now, [A-Z]` (quoted model sentences keep the capital).
- **"a paper" as a countable noun** (L555 poster, L557 card, and L484 earlier): scan for "a big paper", "a small paper", "flat paper".
- **AI starters that make the child the master or the newcomer**: framed as pretend play here ("Let's pretend I'm..."). Watch for similar role lines in L561-600.
- **Lowercase quoted sentences in the teacherScript** ("say, sorry", "ask, whose turn", "ask, any questions?"): the teacherScripts are less consistent than the segments. A regex such as `(say|says|ask|asks|hear), [a-z]` finds them.

- **Beat splits must follow the spelling unit being taught** (L559 "Li-quid. E-qual."): check other hyphen beats so they don't split digraphs.
