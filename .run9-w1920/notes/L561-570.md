These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, OK, US praise, "I say / I ask", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First, / After that, / Finally,", "Last time, we ..." in L562, AI starters with the child partner).
