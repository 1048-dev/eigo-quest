### Run 9 review notes: L571-580 (world 20, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (OK, US praise, "I say", lowercase after "Now, / Then,", L572 seg0 "Last time, we", L578/L580 fall, the "Hi! I'm <partner>." starters). There are 60 patches in 8 lessons. L576 and L578 have no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The English in this world is already clean, so most fixes are small. Themes: (1) "cher" for the -ture sound. TTS reads it like the name Cher (/ʃɛr/), so I changed it to "chur" everywhere in this group (23 places in 8 lessons; same as the W17 fix in L488/L489). (2) Broken fragments that TTS reads with dead stops ("Please come to, on Saturday. It starts at.", "My favorite festival is, because.", "Picture, not pic-ture-e.", "There is is for now"). (3) Capital letters mid-sentence ("OK, Play", "And, Turn", "Quick, Close", "And at the end, Stand") and quoted text that started lowercase. (4) Small wording problems ("Wave your hand to you", "A big land", "A big paper", "says f"). (5) JA that didn't match the EN (きのうは for "Last time", ことしも for "This year", kanji 島). Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 216-230 s long with 98% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### Block-wide notes
- **"cher" -> "chur" for /tʃər/**: world 20 used "cher" (and "Cher, cher, cher!") where W17 uses "chur". I fixed all 23 instances in L571-580. **L591-600 still has 6 "cher"** (grep `\b[Cc]her\b`). Change them to "chur" in the same way so the block stays consistent. "zher" (/ʒər/) and "yoor" are kept. They are in scripts/english-words.txt and read acceptably.
- **"Yesterday" in teacherScripts**: the central fix changed seg0 in L572, but its teacherScript still said "Yesterday, we arrived..." and seg0 subtitleJa still said きのうは (both fixed here). Check other lessons where seg0 was changed (L562) for the same leftovers in teacherScript / subtitleJa.
- **Capital after a comma before a TPR command** ("OK, Play", "And, Turn", "Quick, Close", "And at the end, Stand"): the central fix only covered Now / Then / First / After that / Finally. Other lead-ins slip through. A grep for `, [A-Z][a-z]+ ` on TPR command text in say/subtitleEn will find more.
- **Fragments with dangling prepositions or conjunctions** ("Please come to, on Saturday.", "It starts at.", "My favorite festival is, because."): these come up when a pattern with ～ is read aloud. Scan for `to, |at\. |is, because\.`.
- **phonics.tipJa labels** "-ure の まとめ:" (L575) and "ふりかえり:" (L580) have an ASCII colon (same note as W17). They were not changed.
- **Quiz slides must be whole lines**: when a wrong option is a fragment of a longer line, the slide can show the right answer by accident (L573). Check other quizzes where the slide item is longer than the spoken option.
- Kept on purpose: "Shall we dance?" (target, 中2 standard); L573 "Haru, please invite Riko."; L575 "the fishers"; L576 "A, an adult." option style; L578 "A deer appeared"; L577 JA さけんでる; L579 expressions[1].ja あるよ; L580 Birimo's "Please tell me about a festival." (a child host, polite).
