### Run 9 review notes: L541-550 (world 19, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "Yes, it is. Or no, it isn't." says, OK, US praise, "I say", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First, / After that, / Finally,", AI starters "Hi! I'm <partner>."). There are 69 patches in 10 lessons, so every lesson has at least one change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. The only learning-item field I touched is the Japanese of L541 tpr[3]. The English in this world is clean and the fixes are small. Themes: (1) **the block-wide lowercase rule went too far**. After "First," it also lowercased the quoted order words Next / Then / Finally, so "First, next, Then, finally." now appears in mixed case (L547, L548, L550). (2) TTS problems: "Wind, rewind" (TTS reads bare "wind" as the air word), "El, e, va, tor", "Ree, again!" and "Ree, one more time" (both sound like "say ree again"). (3) Wording or facts a teacher would not use: "These are scissors... It's used for", a smoothie in a mixer, "You can't throw flat paper", "cut the corners" for a pinwheel slit, "Shall we help?", "What do we do finally?". (4) One lowercase "brilliant" that the British-praise fix missed. Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer, and the one over-12 line (the L543 starter) is split. The check script passes: ALL OK. The lessons are 218-227 s long with 97-98% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### Block-wide notes
- **Lowercase-after-order-word rule misfires on quoted order words**: when the order words themselves are being taught ("First, Next, Then, Finally"), the block-wide fix lowercased the word after "First," and left the others capitalized. I fixed L547 seg8/seg20, L548 seg8, and L550 seg1/seg17/seg18. **Please scan all 60 lessons for `First, next` and `Then, finally`** (and `Next, then`, `Then, after that`) in quiz options and "you can use First, ..." sentences. Plain sentences like "First, fold the paper" are correct as they are.
- **"Wind" heteronym**: "Wind, rewind." (L544, L545) was fixed. The learning items "Wind it up." and "Wind it up and let go." (L548 tpr[1], expressions[2], L549 conversation) cannot be changed here. Please check how the TTS reads "Wind it up" (it should be /waɪnd/). If it is wrong, a TTS pronunciation hint is better than changing the item.
- **Lowercase "brilliant"** (L543 seg19) slipped past the British-praise fix, which apparently matched only "Brilliant". Grep the block for a lowercase `brilliant` and `lovely`.
- **"Ree, ..." fragments** (L543 seg9 "Ree, again!", L544 seg9 "Ree, one more time."): these sound like "say ree again". If other groups explain re- the same way, use "Ree means again."
- **Syllable spell-outs**: L548 seg10 "El, e, va, tor" -> "El-e-va-tor" (hyphen beat form, as the brief asks). Check other clap-the-beats lines.
- **くるま for "wheel"** (L541): Japanese kids read くるま as "car". Check other worlds for "wheel" translated as くるま.
