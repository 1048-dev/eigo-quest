These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, OK, US praise, "I say", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First,", "..." in titles, AI starters "Hi! I'm <partner>."). There are 50 patches in 9 lessons. L582 has no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The English in this world is clean and warm, so the fixes are small. Themes: (1) Japanese-style wording ("the kitchen of the hall", "Excuse me" when entering a home, "Think about you", "The fish is first"); (2) explanations that teach only half the rule or that change the story (L588 "right now" next to "every day", "sometimes" instead of "always", "dinner" when the guests had cake); (3) TTS problems in stress spell-outs ("A-BOUT" read with the letter name, "BRA" in "Cel-e-BRA-tion"); (4) British/stiff phrases ("What shall we do?", "Please begin.", "Lovely!" as praise); (5) say/subtitleEn mismatches (L587 seg8, L589 seg8) and three Japanese errors. The one over-12 sentence (L586 lines[5], 13 words) is now 12 words. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 221-227 s long with 98-99% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L581 Welcome to My House
- A: The hosting scene is natural ("Come on in, everyone!", "I feel at home already."). In the teacherScript, "Lovely!" as praise after "Say it with me." is British, and "Guests say, Thank you. Excuse me." is a calque of おじゃまします; an American guest does not say "Excuse me" when walking in. "Thanks for having me" would be natural, but it is the target of L583, so I did not use it. The AI starter has two greetings ("Hi! ... Knock, knock! ... Hello! May I come in?").
- B: "What a lovely house!" and vocab "lovely" are known keepers. Using "Lovely!" as praise in the same lesson blurs the new word. conversation.lines[3].ja says かべの たいこが すてき, but the EN says "I like the drums" (seg7 JA already has すき).
- C: In seg9, "A-BOUT!" will be read with the letter name /eɪ/, which is the opposite of the weak uh the segment teaches. The same segment already says "Uh-bout". In seg6, "I say, My aunt says, make yourself at home." is a little stacked, but it reads OK (single flag, kept).
- Decision: teacherScript "Lovely!" -> "Great!" and "Excuse me." -> "Thank you so much." (A+B). starterEn: drop "Hello!" (A+C). seg9 "A-BOUT!" -> "Uh-BOUT!" (B+C). lines[3].ja すてき -> すき (objective JA).

#### Block-wide notes
- **Stress spell-outs with real vowel letters** (L581 "A-BOUT", L588 "Cel-e-BRA-tion", L584 "Pa-RADE / Bal-LOON / To-DAY"): TTS reads a stressed or capital "A" as /eɪ/ and "BRA" as /brɑ/. I fixed the two clear cases ("Uh-BOUT" uses a form already in the line, and "BRAY" is a real word). The L584 forms are kept because they follow the hyphen beat rule. Check other "-tion" stress lines for "BRA" / "NA" style beats.
- **"J, j, j!" / "Ch, ch, ch!"** (L587 seg9, L586 seg9): TTS will likely read the letter names ("jay"). "juh" is not in scripts/english-words.txt ("tuh", "buh", "puh" are). Consider adding "juh"/"chuh" and changing them all together. Not changed here.
- **"this week" / "Stage 2" in kid-facing text** (L586 teacherScript "This week, let's review Stage 2 spellings", L586-L589 phonics.tipJa "ステージ2 の つづり①②③", L590 seg8 "You learned all of these this week!" / こんしゅう): these assume a weekly schedule and show an internal label. Consider "in this world" / "ワールド20 で". Not changed here.
- **"the kitchen of the hall"-type の-calques**: fixed in L586. Look for "the X of the Y" with places elsewhere.
- **Unattributed lines in the narrated "Let's listen" segments**: Ms. Emma's own lines are fine without "I say". Another character's reply with no name is confusing (fixed in L589 seg7).
- **subtitleEn " / " that is not a quiz**: L589 seg8 had "That's a great idea! / Maybe at lunch." while say had "Or". The block-wide "A / B" subtitle fix may have caught non-quiz lines. Worth a quick grep for " / " in subtitleEn where say has "Or".
- Kept on purpose: L581 vocab "lovely" and "What a lovely house!"; L581 seg6 "I say, My aunt says, ..."; L582 "Yum, what a snack time!" and the "Give me cake!" distractor; L583 Kiko as host in the starter; L584 "Yesterday, Grandpa Toma ... told us a story" (story time) and "my aunt's father"; L585 "Tomorrow is a holiday!"; L586 "Catch the rain in the kitchen!"; L587 scissors safety line placement; L590 "Watch from a safe place with a grown-up."
