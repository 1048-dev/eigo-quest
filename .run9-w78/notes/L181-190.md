#### L181 Hello, New Friends!
- A: The self-introduction dialogue is natural, and so is "You sound like a real islander!". The wrap "You can say My name is, and Nice to meet you." is the same comma chain that was fixed in L142.
- B: The level is right. Echoing "Nice to meet you, too." is taught clearly, and "too は こちらこそ" is a good gloss. The silent s in island is correct. seg17 "Say My name is, and your name." has the same bare-phrase problem.
- C: The commas around "My name is" force odd pauses in seg17 and seg20. Everything else reads well.
- Decision: seg17 and seg20 say/subtitleEn quote the phrases (`Say "My name is" and your name.`, `You can say "My name is" and "Nice to meet you."`) (A+C, same as the L142 fix).

#### L183 I Like Soccer
- A: The likes talk is natural ("I don't like spiders, either!"). seg12 "I say a thing." is not English a teacher would use; it should be "I'll say something.". The quiz choice "Or I don't like?" is not a sentence anyone says. "school terrace" is rare in the US (only A, scene text).
- B: The quiz choice "I don't like" leaves the object out, so the child compares a full chunk with a broken one. It should be "I don't like soccer.". The ay tip (usually at the end of a word) is correct.
- C: "I say a thing." sounds clipped. seg17 "I like, and I don't like." needs the quoting fix.
- Decision: seg12 say/subtitleEn -> "I'll say something." (A+C). seg17 say/subtitleEn -> `Say "I like" and "I don't like."` (A+C). seg18 choice -> "Or I don't like soccer?" in say, subtitleEn and subtitleJa (A+B). The slide item "I don't like ～." stays, because slide items must be lesson items.

#### L188 First, Second, Third
- A: The TPR items "First, raise your hand. / Second, touch your nose. / Third, sit down." mean "to begin / next / then" to a native speaker, not "the first/second/third person". The speed challenge even gives them out of order ("First... Third... Second..."). A US teacher lining kids up says "First in line, raise your hand!".
- B: This is an EN/JA meaning mismatch on items (the JA and the actions say 1ばんめの ひと), and it teaches the wrong use of the ordinals in an ordinals lesson. seg1 subtitleJa "1にち、2にち、3にち、4か、5か" has the wrong date readings: 1にち reads いちにち (one day), not the 1st, and 4か/5か are not words. "From four, just add th" is a simplification (fifth is spelled differently), but it is fine for Stage 1. seg9 "It says kw." will be read as letter names.
- C: "kw" is read by TTS as "K, W". "First in line, raise your hand!" is just as easy to shout.
- Decision: tpr[1]-[3] -> "First in line, raise your hand." / "Second in line, touch your nose." / "Third in line, sit down." (A+B, **item ID change**), plus seg11-13 say, subtitleEn and slides. seg1 subtitleJa -> ついたち、ふつか、みっか、よっか、いつか (objective). seg9 say/subtitleEn "kw" -> "/kw/" (B+C). **titleJa "1にち、2にち、3にち" has the same date error**, so it is flagged for the coordinator.
