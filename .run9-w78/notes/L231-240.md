#### L231 Our Town
- A: "We have a park in our town." / "Let's write it down!" are natural. The wrap line "You can say what your town has with We have, and read igh words." is a little clunky, but you can follow it.
- B: The level is right. igh = /aɪ/ with silent gh is correct. "Our means me and my friends." is a fair simplification for kids, and the JA わたしと ともだちの matches. The quiz distractor "in me town" is a plausible learner error.
- C: "n, igh, t, night!" has the usual TTS risk with letter-name segments (block-wide). Everything else reads well.
- Decision: no change (single flags only).

#### L232 We Don't Have a Zoo
- A: "We don't have an aquarium, either." and Birimo's "That's sad!" are natural. seg0 "Yesterday we wrote..." is fine as English.
- B: seg0 "Yesterday" / きのうは assumes the child did L231 the day before. This is the same issue as L72/L167. The aiTask starter's first sentence has 13 words and is not the taught form.
- C: The aiTask starter is one long breath. The "but" target sentence (10 words) is a coordinator keeper and reads fine.
- Decision: aiTask.starterEn now uses the taught 10-word sentence: "We have a station, but we don't have an airport. Do you have an aquarium in your town?" (B+C). seg0 Yesterday -> "Last time," and きのうは -> まえの レッスンでは (objective+B).

#### L233 I Want a Pool in Our Town
- A: The dream-town meeting is natural ("Great ideas! Let's put them on our map."). "A light in your head" for idea would usually be "a lightbulb", but it is OK. "Super thinking!" is fine.
- B: ie = /aɪ/ (pie, tie, lie, fries) is correct, and so is the link to igh. "Why? ― I like swimming." is a fine Stage 1 reason (no "because" yet).
- C: The script reads well. "Snip!" and "Higher and higher!" have a good rhythm.
- Decision: no change.

#### L234 Our Town Is Nice
- A: "Today we're writing the good points of our town." is a calque of いいところ. The wrap "tell fun things with You can" is not English. The aiTask "What is your town like?" sounds stiff in speech. "You can enjoy shopping at the market." is textbook-like but acceptable (expression ID). "The skies are clear." is poetic, but it carries the phonics word.
- B: Same flag on "good points" and "tell fun things". The tip "y -> ie + s (sky -> skies)" is correct. The seg9 JA "sky に s が つくと skies" simplifies this but is not wrong.
- C: "You can say Our town is nice, and tell fun things with You can. And you can..." has three unmarked "you can"s and is hard to phrase. "What's your town like?" flows better than "What is".
- Decision: seg0 say/subtitleEn -> "Today we're writing what's great about our town." (A+B). aiTask + seg17 say/subtitleEn -> "What's your town like?" (A+C). seg20 say/subtitleEn -> `You can say "Our town is nice." You can talk about fun things with "You can."` (A+B+C, the same quoting as the L142 wrap fix).

#### L235 Town Guidebook

- B: igh vs ie spelling review is clear and correct. "Sharp eyes!" fits the JA すごい めだね / よく みてたね. The wrap sentence has 15 words.

- Decision: seg19 "Amazing eyes!" -> "Sharp eyes!" (A+B). seg20 say/subtitleEn -> "You can say what your town has and doesn't have. And what you can do there!" (A+B+C).

#### L236 My Hero Speech
- A: "That's a lovely speech." leans British, like the "Lovely manners!" fix; a US teacher says "wonderful". The seg1 list "Who, what kind, can do, and helps." is not something anyone says.
- B: The seg9 phonics claim "Or a short u, like foot" is wrong. Short u is /ʌ/ (cup), and foot has /ʊ/, which L211-220 teach as "short oo". In seg17, "He is... She can..." mixes pronouns for one hero. In seg7, Ms. Emma's own reply has no "And I say", so it can sound like Birimo thanking himself.
- C: The seg1 fragment list is hard to read aloud. The seg17 pronoun switch sounds like a slip. Ms. Emma's reply in seg7 needs a lead-in.
- Decision: conversation line 4, seg7 say/subtitleEn/slide -> "That's a wonderful speech." (A+B). seg7 adds "And I say," and the subtitleJa adds そして わたし「…」 (B+C). seg1 -> "Who is it? What are they like? What can they do? How do they help?" (A+C). seg9 "a short u" -> "a short oo" (objective+B). seg17 -> "He is... or she is... He can... or she can..." (B+C, the block form), and subtitleJa uses "He / She".
