# Run 9 review notes: L281-290 (world 10, Stage 1)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, testPoints, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not repeat the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "A? Or b?" quiz says, US praise, Ding-dong, showtime, "Now, point", "やあ、みんな！", きのう/あした), and I did not undo any of them. There are 85 patches, and all 10 lessons change (L281-L290). No lesson is unchanged. No learning item IDs change: no vocab, expression, TPR or phonics strings are touched. The only item-level change is the Japanese gloss of L289 expression[2] ("こどもに たのしいです。" -> "こどもが たのしめます。"), which is not an ID. Three conversation lines change (L283 lines[3], L285 lines[3], L286 lines[1]-[2]), and the slide items that repeat them are updated. No test or code in tests/, src/ or scripts/ uses these strings. Every new sentence has 8 words or fewer. The check script passes (ALL OK): the lessons are 217-228 s long with 87-88% English.

## L281 Life Around the World
- A: The food fair and "In India, people eat curry." are natural. In seg4, "Tacos. Fold it like this" uses "it" for a plural word. The wrap "You can say In India, people eat curry." is the bare-phrase pattern, and "You can tell about pasta" is not natural ("talk about"). "They look funny and yummy!" is a little odd for tacos, but kids say it and funny is a phonics word.
- B: The level is right. "Not everyone, but many people" is a good, honest gloss of "people". The -y /i/ words are correct for w10Phonics. The "it" after "Tacos" may confuse learners about singular and plural. seg8 "Start with In and a country. Then people, and what they do." has unquoted pattern words.
- C: seg8 and seg19 force odd pauses around the bare pattern words. "h, a, pp, y" is the usual segment pattern. The rest reads well.
- Decision: seg4 say/subtitleEn "Fold it" -> "Fold one" (A+B). seg8 say/subtitleEn quote "In" and "people" (A+C, block rule 11). seg19 say/subtitleEn quote the target and "tell about" -> "talk about" (A+C).

## L286 I Recommend This Book

"Make yourself little." would be "small" for a native speaker, but little is the phonics word and an item ID (A only). seg8 "To ask a friend, What do you recommend?" is missing a verb.
- B: Riko's line "It's about a little turtle and its trip around the world." has 11 words, and its/it's in one line is a trap for learners. It is not the target, so it should be shortened. The -le /əl/ tip (the final e is silent) is correct.

- Decision: line[1] -> "What's it about?" (A+C). line[2] -> "It's about a little turtle. It travels the world!" (A+B), with ja "ちいさな カメの おはなし。せかいを たびするの！".
