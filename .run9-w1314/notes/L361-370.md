### Run 9 review notes: L361-370 (world 13, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "A... or b..." says, US praise, "Ding-dong!", "Give a thumbs-up.", lowercase after "Now,", "fisher", JA say lines ending in "."). There are 47 patches in 8 lessons. L361 and L368 have no changes. There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The most common fix is the simple present used for an action happening right now in conversation lines ("That seagull watches my sandwich!", "Look, she dances with the ball!", "I help here today"). Other fixes: quotes with no speaker verb, formula chains in "Here's the point", unclear or clipped teacher lines ("Not hello!", "Almost ts.", "The end S"), and a few JA meaning or naturalness errors (それで, どこにも, クッキーを ねがう). Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 219-228 s long with 98% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L364 Riko Has a Notebook
- A: Kiko says "Riko has a notebook. What do you write in it?" to Riko herself, so she talks about Riko in the third person to her face. "Look, Riko has a notebook!" makes the first part a remark to the others. "That's very polite" for "Can I see it?" overstates a little (single flag, kept).
- B: The JA リコ、ノート もってるね addresses Riko, so it does not match the EN either way. The has/writes/wants point is clear. In seg9, "Almost ts." is not a clear instruction.
- C: TTS will read "ts" as letters or a non-word.
- Decision: conversation.lines[0] en/ja, seg6 say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa/slide -> "Look, Riko has a notebook! What do you write in it?" / みて、リコが ノートを もってる！ (A+B). seg9 "Almost ts." -> "The T and S go together.", JA follows (A+C).
