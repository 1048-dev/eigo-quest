### Run 9 review notes: L371-380 (world 13, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "A... or b..." says, OK, US praise, "Ding-dong!", lowercase after "Now,") or the central decisions ("fisher", "Well done!", the "t c h" spell-outs, Japanese inside `say` ending with "."). There are 46 patches in 9 lessons (L378 has no change). There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The most common fixes are telegraphic or formula lines in "Here's the point" ("In the answer, one person, add S.", "Answer with He's a, and the job.", "What time does she, then the verb with no s."), "Think of anyone, a friend..." in the Your-turn segments, "tell about your day" (-> talk about), and odd playful closers ("Yummy work today!", "What a sunrise lesson!"). Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 211-226 s long with 97-98% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L371 I Always Brush My Teeth
- A: The morning routine is natural ("Rise and shine!", "Where's my comb?"). seg8 "Here is the point." differs from every other lesson ("Here's the point."). seg20 "what you always do and usually do" is clumsy. Birimo addressing Kiko and then talking about her in the third person is a little odd, but it is a joke and recycles the target sentence (kept).
- B: The y -> ies rule and "always/usually before the action" are clear and one step at a time. EN and JA match. "eez / eyes" as sound hints are fine.
- C: seg0 "It is morning" is stiff to read; "It's morning" flows. seg8 "Here is" also reads stiffly. seg20 needs the second "what" for rhythm.
- Decision: seg0 "It is" -> "It's" (A+C). seg8 "Here is the point." -> "Here's the point." (A+C). seg20 -> "what you always do and what you usually do" (A+C).

#### L373 What Time Does She Get Up?
- A: The bakery story is natural. The teacherScript formula "say, What time does she, then the verb with no s." is not teacher talk. "Think of anyone, a friend or a family member." is unnatural. "Yummy work today!" is not an English praise phrase. "Thank you, baker!" is cute (kept).
- B: does/goes/has are taught clearly, and "duz, not dooz" is helpful. In seg8, "In the answer, one person, add S." is a fragment. The JA おいしい がんばり is odd Japanese.
- C: The teacherScript chain and the seg8 fragment cannot be phrased naturally.
- Decision: teacherScript -> "To ask about time, use does. The verb has no s." (A+C). seg8 -> "In the answer, she is one person, so add S." (A+C). seg17 -> "Think of someone, like a friend or a family member." (A+B). seg20 "Yummy work today!" -> "Great work today!", JA きょうも よく がんばったね！ (A+B).
