### Run 9 review notes: L961-970 (world 33, Stage 4)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (US praise, I say / I ask, Hi / Hello openings, "Let's pretend I'm" starters, fall). There are 45 patches, and every lesson has at least one. There are no learning-item changes: school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en and phonics words are untouched, so no test or src line is affected. The grammar order is clean. No new text adds make sure / hold on / stay calm / as soon as / unless / rebuilt, and the safety word list is respected. Themes: (1) **Bare letter groups that TTS misreads**: "Add ion" (L961, L963), "Add ly" and "u r e" (L964), "Add ance" / "Keep ance" (L966), "ent / ence / ance" (L967), "Ency sounds like en see" (L968), "Add ty" (L969). They are now spelled as letters (the teacherScripts already did this) or carried by a real word. (2) **Facts and safety**: the L967 typhoon card said only "It is calm there" about the eye. It now adds "but the storm comes back soon", so a child does not go outside in the eye. L966 "fix tall furniture to the wall" means "repair" to a US child, so it is now "attach". (3) **Fragments left by the block-wide I-say swap**: L969 "I practice." and L970 "I read." (4) **Small fixes**: the L961 answer had no speaker; the L963 "When, then a comma" rule was wrong; the L969 chime is now "Ding-dong" everywhere (as the starter has it); L970 "there was no absence"; JA "あつくて あつい" (L962) and "ほんの すこしの センチメートル" (L965). All facts checked are right: four layers, a thin crust like apple skin, a thick hot mantle, the inner core about as hot as the sun's surface, magma vs lava, Hawaii made by volcanoes, geothermal energy, plates moving a few cm a year like fingernails, four plates and about 111 active volcanoes in Japan, typhoons forming over warm water in summer and fall, tsunami as a series of waves that can travel as fast as a jet in deep ocean (go at once, never to the beach, stay until the warning is over), and 119 for fire / ambulance.
