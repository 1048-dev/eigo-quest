### Run 9 review notes: L1011-1020 (world 34, Stage 4)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (US praise, I say / I ask, Hi / Hello openings, "Let's pretend I'm" starters). There are 94 patches in all 10 lessons; no lesson is left with no change. Most of the patches are the UK/US policy in L1011-1014. **Three learning items change because the policy requires it**: L1012 school.expressions[2].en "Could we have the bill, please?" -> "Could we have the check, please?", L1014 school.expressions[2].en "... It's in the Science Centre, on the second floor." -> "Science Center", and L1013 tpr[0].en "Press the button for the lift!" -> "Press the button for the elevator!". In each case I also changed every field that has the same text (grammar, conversation, aiTask target, goalJa, recap, slides, say/subtitleEn). No test or src line pins any of these (grep of tests/ and src/ for "bill, please", "Science Cent", "button for the lift": no hits). The core expressions (expressions[0]) are not touched. No phonics word changes. The grammar order is clean: no new text adds "would you mind", "recommend", "I'm afraid", "instead", "I'm writing to" or other order-pinned forms. Themes: (1) **UK/US policy**: British forms kept only as the UK side of a comparison, as the phonics word, or in Mrs. Brown's words. Brightwater signs, menus and brochures (L1011 square sign, L1012 menu, L1014 price list, L1017 brochure) and Ms. Emma's own words (L1012 the check, L1016 email "lovely holiday", L1018 "favourite part") now use US forms. (2) **Story timeline**: L1013 put the hotel meeting "this evening", but L1014 (the museum, "today") and L1015 (Clock Tower, boat, lunch and museum on one day) come after it. I moved it to the afternoon. (3) **TTS cues**: comma-split capital syllables ("EL, e, va, tor", "Va, CA, tion", "Fif, TEEN", "Eigh, TEEN") now use the hyphen beat form, and the bare "ize" in "Final / Modern / Real plus ize" is now "i, z, e" (as the -ize reviews already say). (4) **Small fixes**: JA ミスター・リー -> リーさん, the L1015 JA こんばんは under "Hello", praise said twice in a row, L1015 "Read two numbers at a time" for 760, L1019 keychain "toy". The checker passes with ALL OK. All lessons are 208-225 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.
