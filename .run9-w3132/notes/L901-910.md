These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (US praise, I say / I ask, Hi / Hello openings, "Let's pretend I'm" starters). There are 32 patches in 7 lessons; L903, L905 and L907 have no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words, so no test or src line is affected. The grammar order is clean: "used to" starts in L902, "was/were built / invented / made / found / used" in L906 and later only (L903 "The telephone was a big improvement" and L904 "are made of" do not match the rule), and no new text adds an order-pinned form. Themes: (1) **Phonics spelling rules**: L901 taught "Add t, i, o, n" to invent / protect / collect / connect, which would give "inventtion"; the verbs already end in t, so the ending added is i, o, n (src/lib/curriculum/w31Phonics.ts already says "invent ＋ ion"). Fixed in the teacherScript, seg10 and tipJa. (2) **Letter groups and stress cues TTS misreads**: L902 "Make A strong" (read as "make a strong"), L904 "Say al softly" ("Al"), L908 "Add the and an s". (3) **Wording a child would not follow**: L906 "Read years two numbers at a time", L909 "Now is the twenty-first century" and "The number is one ahead", L910 "Haru guides the castle model" (you guide people, not things). (4) **Small consistency fixes**: L902 Riko's retold line dropped today's "used to"; L908 seg8 JA kept エマ先生は にっこり although the EN is "I say"; praise repeated in back-to-back segments (L904 "Super!" / "Super job!", L910 "Great guiding!" / "Great guides!"). All facts checked are right (Meiji 1868, Tokyo Olympics 1964, Bell 1876, Wright brothers 1903 and about twelve seconds, Swan and Edison improving the bulb, rope-pattern clay pots about 5,000 years old, the century = hundreds + 1 rule). Every new sentence has 13 words or fewer. The check script passes with ALL OK. All touched lessons are 215-228 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L901 The Corridor of Time
- A: The stone stairs, Old Tock as a walking grandfather clock and the Time Map note are warm and natural. "Make up a fun name. ... I'm Captain Gear" is a good Your turn. Nothing sounds translated.
- B: Each + singular verb (opens, with an s) is explained clearly. The phonics rule is wrong: teacherScript "Add t, i, o, n, and say shun", seg10 "Add t, i, o, n to an action word" and tipJa "どうしに -tion を つけると" all say we add -tion to invent / protect / collect / connect, which would spell "inventtion". The verbs end in t; we add -ion (w31Phonics.ts: "invent ＋ ion").
- C: Reads well. "shun" is a real word and TTS reads it close to /ʃən/ (kept). "Tick, tock" is the keeper's call (kept).
- Decision: teacherScript -> "These verbs end in t. Add i, o, n, and t, i, o, n says shun."; seg10 (say, subtitleEn) -> "Sound time! These action words end in t. Add i, o, n, and they become things. Say t, i, o, n softly, shun." with JA to match; tipJa -> "t で おわる どうしに -ion を つけると、-tion で おわる ..." (objective). testPoints[1] ("-tion（どうしを なまえの ことばに …）") names the ending only and is kept.
