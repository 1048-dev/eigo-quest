#### L911 She Was Born in a Small Village
- A: The Biography Door, the giant picture book and Stella's village ("one road, one shop, and many sheep") are lovely, and "made up" is in the teacherScript (tested). seg8 "Was born is always in the past." is not what a teacher says as a grammar point.
- B: Same seg8 flag. The point should show the form (was + born), because Japanese learners say "I born in ~". The JA つくりの ひと is not natural Japanese. seg2 defines a biography as "a real person's life" while Stella is made up, but the teacherScript and conversation say clearly that her story is made up (kept). The JA でんき (biography) is the same word as でんき (electricity) in L919, but the vocab JA explains it (note only).
- C: seg7 "Kiko smiles. There were no city lights..." has no "says". It still reads well because the line is true as narration too (single flag, kept). "so she could see so many stars" repeats so (single flag, kept; it is a conversation line).
- Decision: seg8 (say, subtitleEn) -> "Here's the point. We say was born, not just born. Stella is made up, but her story feels real." and JA -> born だけ では なく「was born」と いうよ。ステラは つくりばなしの ひと だけど、… (A+B, objective JA).
