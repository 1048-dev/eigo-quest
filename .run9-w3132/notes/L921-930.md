### Run 9 review notes: L921-930 (world 31, Stage 4)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (US praise, I say / I ask, Hi / Hello openings, "Let's pretend I'm" starters). There are 64 patches in 8 lessons; L922 and L928 have no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words, so no test or src line is affected. The grammar order is clean: "I respect" first appears in L922 and "I'd like to talk about" in L925, and no new passive, past perfect or "what if" form is added anywhere. Themes: (1) **Missing speaker tags in segments** (L921 seg7, L925 seg6 and seg8, L926 seg7, L927 seg6 and seg7, L929 seg7, L930 seg7): a quoted line came straight after narration ("Haru presses the button. Buzz! It was in nineteen oh three."), so TTS reads it as the narrator's words. I added "He asks," / "and says," the way the teacherScript does. (2) **Story and fact errors**: L925 seg7 gave Birimo's two reasons about Old Tock to Haru's grandpa; in the L927 teacherScript Kiko answers the moon question, but in the conversation and seg8 Riko does (now Kiko answers the telephone question, Riko the moon question, Birimo the bonus question, so the 2-2 tie adds up); "When were the Tokyo Olympics held? 1964" ignores the 2021 Games (now "the first Tokyo Olympics"); L930 defined a desert as "sandy" just before W32 (most deserts are not sand). (3) **Suffix wording for TTS**: "Invent plus ion", "Celebrate plus ion", "Explore plus ation", "a noun with tion" and "Ex, plo, RA, tion!" (L927, L929, L930). TTS reads "ion" as eye-on, and the target /shun/ ending gets lost. I used L901's own wording ("Say the end softly, shun"), L902's spelling ("a, t, i, o, n") and the hyphen beat form "Ex-plo-RA-tion". (4) **Vague rules**: "The number is one ahead" for centuries (L926 seg9, L930 seg9, seg19) is now "Nineteen is one more than eighteen" / "Twenty-one is one more than twenty"; L929 seg8 "Was built, things" is now "something people made". (5) **Small wording fixes**: frame names like "Ask, When did you? and Why did you?" (L924 seg1, L927 seg20), the L923 quiz "Where is a harbor for airplanes?" with place names as answers, the L926 "I never give up on my dream!" model, the L927 "Be the quiz host" model that asks about the child's own birthday party, and the double "Next" in the L930 wrap-up. Every new sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes with ALL OK. All touched lessons are 216-228 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.
