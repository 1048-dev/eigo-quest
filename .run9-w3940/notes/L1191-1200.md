#### Block-wide notes
- **Quotes with no "says"**: this is very common in W40 story segments ("X smiles. <quote>", "X points. <quote>", "X yawns. <quote>", a reply with no speaker after a question). grep say for `\. (Me|Wait|Sure|Because|Good point|Oh no)` and "X <verb>s\. [A-Z]" in L1171-1190. The teacherScript has the same pattern ("Birimo smiles shyly. I forgot!" L1195, "Birimo stands up proudly. I believe" L1198, "Haru smiles. This is not the end." L1200). I left those because TTS reads `say`.
- **Bare sound spellings**: "er", "ar", "ay", "ir", "ur", "Im", "ness" in say. W40 reviews (L1175, 1180, 1185, 1190) may have "Say er" or "ends with ay". Use real words (her, car) or spelled letters.
- **"FU" stress**: if another speech-pause drill isolates a stressed syllable that is a rude letter pair, describe it ("the start of future") and keep the capital form in the display.
- **lead / tear / read in the tricky-sounds drills**: give context for heteronyms ("lead, as in lead the way", "tear, as in teardrop").
- **"another continent" / where the children are from**: watch W39-W40 for claims that Riko / Haru / Kiko are on a different continent from Priya (Asia).

(L1191 line 7), "like a big yawn" (L1193), ひと休み for pause, しんじて います, "Goodnight!", "The Ray of Thanks / Beginnings lit up!", the capital stress "In the FUture" / "ToGETHer" / "Thanks to YOU", "goed" (L1197), and Ms. Emma in the cards and speeches ("Dear Ms. Emma", "Most of all, Ms. Emma taught us", "With love, Ms. Emma").
