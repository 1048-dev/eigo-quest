#### Block-wide notes
- **Quotes with no "says" / answers with no speaker**: this is the main pattern in W39 Talk segments (seg6-8): "X smiles / sighs / laughs / nods / worries / begins. <quote>", and "Question? Answer." where the answer belongs to a child. Grep say for `\. (Kiko|Riko|Haru|Birimo) (smiles|laughs|sighs|nods|gasps|worries|begins)\. ` and for "? I learned / I agree / I think" with no speaker. It probably recurs in L1171-1200 too.
- **Frame sentences read aloud** ("Then, However, and your worry", "Today, we'll talk about, and the topic"): these "Here's the point" segments use a comma-list of phrase + "and the ~" that TTS cannot phrase. Give a full model sentence, or use "say X".
- **Bare letter groups**: I followed the L1141-1150 group (a, t, i, o, n / a, l / e, d; "En" and "Re" kept). Other review lessons that say "plus ation / plus al / plus ed" should match.
