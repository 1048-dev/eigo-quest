### Run 9 review notes: L301-310 (world 11, Stage 1)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles and "A... or b..." says, US praise, "Ding-dong!", "Give a thumbs-up.", lowercase after "Now,"). There are 33 patches in 9 lessons (L301, L303-L310). L302 has no changes. No learning item IDs change. The most common fix is again the "formula read aloud as a comma chain" pattern in "Here's the point" ("This is me at, then the place", "We had, then the event", "Who said it, then said, then the words."). I replaced these with model sentences, and the subtitleJa keeps the formula. Every new English sentence has 8 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK (215-227 s, 88-90% English, every slide item valid).

#### L301 What's Your Best Memory?
- A: The album talk is natural ("Haru, what's your best memory?", "You jumped so high! Like a frog!"). In seg8, "Answer with My best memory is, then one thing." is a formula, not teacher talk. In seg20, "answer My best memory is." stops on "is".
- B: The -ed /t/ rule (after p or k) is correct for the four words. EN and JA match. The seg8 model is clearer as a full sentence. The quiz slide shows "Jump high." for option B "jump" (the bare word is not a lesson item). This is a small mismatch, so I kept it.
- C: The seg8 chain and the seg20 "...answer My best memory is." cannot be read naturally. The voice drops on "is".
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "Say, My best memory is our school trip. Then add what you did. We walked to an old castle." (A+B+C). seg20 say/subtitleEn -> "You can ask and answer, What's your best memory?" (A+C).

#### L302 My Best Memory Is Sports Day
- A: Natural ("Look! This is our sports day photo.", "Our team won!", "Wipe, wipe, shiny!"). "Sports day" is the Japan-English term (US: field day), but it is a vocab ID used all through the world, and it fits 運動会.
- B: The -ed /d/ rule (after y, n, l) is correct. "Feel your throat buzz." is a good cue. "Then say what was fun, I enjoyed the relay." uses a light comma before a model and is OK.
- C: Reads well. The quiz "play-ed, with two beats" is clear.
- Decision: no change.

#### L303 We Visited Kyoto
- A: "Pack your bags, because today we go on a trip, in our album!" is one long comma-spliced line. In seg8, "What did you do asks about the past." makes the question the subject without quotes, so it sounds broken.
- B: The /ɪd/ rule after t or d is correct. "A king might live here!" with JA とのさま is a fair cultural swap. EN and JA match.
- C: Flags both lines: there is no natural place to breathe in seg0, and seg8 needs question intonation. "It says id" may be read as "I.D." by some TTS voices (single flag, see coordinator).
- Decision: seg0 say/subtitleEn -> "Pack your bags! Today we go on a trip in our album!" (A+C). seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "What did you do? It asks about the past." (A+C).

#### L304 My Album Page
- A: The library whisper opening and Kiko's page are natural ("You smiled a lot!", "Great page, Kiko! Who's next?"). The seg8 line "This is me at, then the place or the event. Add how it was, It was fun!" is a chain. For a school おんがくかい, a US teacher would say "school concert", but "music festival" is a vocab ID (single flag).
- B: The seg8 chain is the key point but hard to follow. A model sentence is clearer. The three -ed sounds review is correct (danced = t).
- C: The seg8 chain cannot be phrased. The rest reads well ("Tiny t, washed. Buzzy d, smiled.").
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "Say, This is me at the music festival. Then add how it was. It was fun!" (A+B+C).

#### L305 Graduation Album
- A: The wrap-up dialogue is natural. "Glue the photo." is a little short (US "Glue the photo down."), but it is a TPR ID (single flag). In seg8, "First, my best memory is." is a fragment.
- B: Same flag on seg8, with a lowercase "my" inside the formula. The quiz (I play the drums yesterday / I played the drums) is a good past-tense check. JA ならも (also Nara) adds a light "also" that links to Kyoto in L303, so I kept it.
- C: The seg8 line stops on "is" and cannot be read naturally.
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "Say your best memory first. My best memory is the music festival. Then add a past sentence. I played the drums." (A+B+C).

#### L306 We Had Sports Day
- A: The dialogue is natural ("How was it?", "I want to run again in junior high!"). "Hands in, go team!" is very American. In seg8, "We had, then the event. It was, then a feeling" is a chain.
- B: The was/saw letter-order point is useful. "The A sounds almost like o" mirrors the JA 「オ」に ちかい and is OK for Japanese learners. The seg8 model sentences make the event / feeling split clear.
- C: The seg8 chain is unreadable. The rest is fine.
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "We had sports day. That's the event. It was exciting. That's the feeling." (A+B+C).

#### L307 The Races Were Exciting
- A: The video scene is natural ("Grab some popcorn.", "My shoes were so muddy after the race!"). In seg8, "The dance, just one, so was. ... We is many people, so were." sounds like broken English. In seg10, "Let's read some friends." sounds like reading people.
- B: The was/were rule (one vs more, and we -> were) is the lesson's key point and must sound correct. "We means many people" keeps the JA we は たくさんの ひと. "Two Es, but just a short were" is a fair simplification.
- C: "We is" will be heard as an error. "read some friends" is a stumble.
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "One dance, so we say was. Many races, so we say were. We means many people, so it's were, too." (A+B+C). seg10 "some friends" -> "some word friends" (A+C).

#### L308 Our Teacher Said Well Done
- A: The chorus talk is natural ("Yes, I said that. Your voices were beautiful."). In seg1, "Then tell what someone said, Our teacher said, Well done!" chains two commas. "Hands together" for "Say thank you." is a Japanese gesture, but it is only the action note (single flag). "More friends." has the same issue as L307.
- B: In seg9, "A I, but not ay. ... Say is say, but said is said!" is circular and does not show the contrast. Better to say it looks like say but has a short e. The seg8 "Who said it, then said, then the words." is a formula chain.
- C: TTS reads "A I" as "AI". The seg8 chain and "Say is say, but said is said!" are hard to say well.
- Decision: seg1 split -> "Then tell what someone said. Our teacher said, Well done!" (A+C). seg8 -> "Who said it? Our teacher. Then add said and the words. Our teacher said, Well done!" (A+B+C). seg9 -> "It looks like say. But it has a short e sound. Said. Say, said. Hear the difference?" (B+C). seg10 "More friends." -> "More word friends." (A+C).

#### L309 There Were Many People
- A: Natural ("I missed the school festival. Tell me about it!", "We sold cookies."). In seg1, "Many, There were many people." hangs. In seg5, "A big paper on the wall." is a countability error.
- B: The there/where/here contrast is correct. The seg1 cue questions ("One stage? ... Many people? ...") match the JA 1つなら / たくさんなら. The seg8 "One stage, There was. Many people, There were." is parallel and short. It reads as a chant, so I kept it.
- C: Same flag on seg1. "Bite your tongue softly for th." is fine.
- Decision: seg1 say/subtitleEn -> "One stage? There was a big stage. Many people? There were many people." (A+C). seg5 -> "A big sheet of paper on the wall." (objective).

#### L310 Sports Day News
- A: "Extra, extra! Read all about it!" and "Front page news!" are fun and natural. In seg8, "Good news has steps." means happy news, not a news story, and the "What happened, We had." chain is a formula.
- B: Same flags. Model answers for the three steps make the review clear. The subtitleJa いい ニュースには had to follow the new English. "Newspaper. Big paper with stories and pictures." is OK as a fragment (single flag).
- C: The seg8 chain cannot be phrased naturally. With question + answer pairs it reads well.
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "Tell your news in order. What happened? We had sports day. How was it? It was exciting. Who was there? There were many families." subtitleJa いい ニュースには じゅんばんが あるよ -> ニュースは じゅんばんに つたえよう (A+B+C).

#### For the coordinator
- **The "formula read aloud" comma chains continue in world 11** ("Here's the point" in almost every lesson: L301, L304, L305, L306, L307, L308, L310). Expect the same template in L311-360, also in "Today's goal" lines ("One thing, There was...").
- **"Let's read some friends." / "More friends."** (phonics word lists) were changed to "word friends" in L307 and L308. Grep the other blocks for "read some friends" and "More friends.".
- **TTS: "A I"** when spelling letters (L308 "A I, but not ay") is read as "AI". Watch for spelled-out "A I" elsewhere. "It says id" (L303) may be read as "I.D." by some voices. I kept it, because it is the same "want, id, wanted" breakdown pattern that earlier blocks kept. Please check it with the real TTS voice.
- **The seg20 recap pattern** "You can ask X, and answer Y." can end on a fragment (L301 "answer My best memory is."). I fixed L301 only. L303/L305 ("You can ask What did you do, and say We visited Kyoto.") have no "?" but read fine, so I kept them.
