### Run 9 review notes: L321-330 (world 11, Stage 1)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles and "A... or b..." says, US praise, "Ding-dong!", "Give a thumbs-up.", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next,", L330 つぎは). There are 35 patches in 6 lessons (L322, L323, L325, L326, L328, L329). L321, L324, L327 and L330 have no changes. One learning item changes: L325 school.expressions[2] (see "ID changes"). The most common fix is again the "formula read aloud" pattern: frames that end on a function word ("Thank you for. I would like to.", "My best memory is.", "... a teacher, because."). I replaced these with "..." fill-in pauses or model sentences, and the subtitleJa keeps its formula where it still matches. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 212-226 s long with 88-90% English.

#### L321 Thank You for Your Help
- A: The thank-you card scene is natural ("Good job, Kiko! You did it.", "For me? I'm going to cry!"). "I could swim 25 meters!" for a single achievement would usually be "I can swim 25 meters now!" or "I swam 25 meters!", but "I could ～." is the lesson's grammar target (school.grammar, aiTask, recap).
- B: The could = できた framing follows the Japanese Grade 6 curriculum. The tipJa "would・should も おなじ -ould で「ウッド」" describes the shared ending, not a full reading of should. That is loose but not wrong. EN and JA match.
- C: Reads well. "Wiggle, wiggle!" and "Shh!" are easy to say.
- Decision: no change. The could point is kept on purpose (see coordinator).

#### L322 I Would Like to Say Thank You
- A: "Excuse me, Ms. Emma. I would like to say thank you." is polite and fine for a child going into a staff room. "At the teachers' room, which is more polite?" should be "In the teachers' room". "teachers' room" is a Japan-specific room (US: "teachers' lounge" / "the office"), but it is a vocab ID and matches 職員室.
- B: The want to / would like to contrast is clear and useful. In seg18, "In" is the preposition to model for being inside a room. はなたば for "flowers" is fine in context ("these flowers").
- C: "With friends, I want to is fine." is a mention phrase, but it reads OK. "I'm smiling from ear to ear." is fine.
- Decision: seg18 say/subtitleEn "At the teachers' room" -> "In the teachers' room" (A+B).

#### L323 Because You Were Kind
- A: The park dialogue is natural ("Why? I didn't do anything special.", "I'm happy because you said that."). In seg8, "I'm happy, because you were kind to me." has a comma that US writing does not use before a final because-clause.
- B: The same seg8 line differs from the lesson expression (no comma) and from slide items[1]. Children copy the written form. "but" listed among reason words (seg10 JA りゆうの ことば) is loose but minor.
- C: "Be, cause. Two beats." is fine. The comma in seg8 adds an unneeded pause.
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa remove the comma (A+B).

#### L324 A Message for Our Teacher

- B: The grammar frame, the vocab and the TPR are item IDs that other lessons reuse (L325 "Give the message board.", L330 Riko). Changing them would be a big ID change for a small gain. EN and JA match.
- C: Reads well.
- Decision: no change. Kept on purpose (see coordinator).

#### L325 Thank-You Party
- A: "We could do our best because you helped us." is a direct calque of がんばれた. Affirmative could for one past achievement sounds like "we would be able to". After Ms. Emma's "You all did your best.", a child says "We did our best because you helped us." In seg8, "Thank you for. I would like to." sounds cut off.
- B: Same flag on could. In this lesson, "could" is not a grammar target (school.grammar is Thank you for / I would like to / because). It stays a sight word in phonics, so the change does not touch the phonics goal. JA せんせいが たすけてくれたので がんばれました still matches.
- C: The seg8 frames drop in pitch on "for" and "to". "Or B, sayed, like day." could be misread by TTS, but it is a quiz distractor and the JA セイド supports it (single flag).
- Decision: school.expressions[2] and every quote of it (conversation.lines[3], seg7 say/subtitleEn/slide, seg8 slide, seg16 say/subtitleEn/slide) -> "We did our best because you helped us." (A+B). seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "Thank you for... I would like to..." (A+C).
