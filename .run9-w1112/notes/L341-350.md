### Run 9 review notes: L341-350 (world 12, Stage 1)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, US praise, "Ding-dong!", "Give a thumbs-up.", lowercase after "Now,", the L349 autumn -> fall / August change). There are 42 patches in all 10 lessons. No learning item IDs change: the only conversation line I changed that a slide quotes is L342 lines[4], and I updated that slide item with it. The most common fixes are formula comma chains in "Here's the point" and wrap-up lines ("I went to, and I played.", "It was, plus fun, exciting or great."), which I replaced with "..." pauses or model sentences, and "tell/write your day", which became "talk/write about". The check script passes: ALL OK. The lessons are 215-229 s long with 89-90% English.

#### L341 Busy Junior High Mornings
- A: The alarm-clock opener and the dialogue are natural ("I get up at ten!" is a good Birimo joke). "The action, then at, then the time." is a formula, not teacher talk.
- B: The magic-e tip is correct, and the at + time rule is the key point. A plain rule ("Put at before the time.") plus the model is clearer. The subtitleJa should follow the new rule.
- C: "then at, then the time" is hard to phrase, because "at" stands alone. Everything else reads well.
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "Put at before the time."; subtitleJa -> じかんの まえに at を つけるよ (A+C).
