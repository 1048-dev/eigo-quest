### Run 9 review notes: L351-360 (world 12, Stage 1)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, lowercase after "Now, / Then,", US praise, "Ding-dong!" for doorbells, "She likes... / He likes..." subtitle). There are 62 patches in all 10 lessons. No learning item IDs change (no vocab, expression, TPR, phonics or target-expression text was touched). L355 swaps two conversation lines (speaker, en and ja together). The most common fixes are formulas read aloud in "Here's the point" / goal segments ("Answer both with It's in.", "My best memory is. We went to.", "I am, I can, my best memory is, and thank you.") and unquoted sentences in the seg20 wrap ("You can ask Who is your hero? and What is..."). I quoted those, like the L189/L204/L216 wrap fixes. The check script passes: ALL OK. The lessons are 215-230 s long with 89-91% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L351 Who and What Questions
- A: The interview talk is natural ("Bread, of course!", "Excuse me, I have a question."). seg8 "Then answer with My hero is, or It's." is a formula, not teacher talk. The seg20 "You can ask Who is your hero? and What is your favorite food?" runs two questions into one sentence.
- B: The who = person / what = thing rule is clear and the JA matches. The who/how and what/want comparison is good. A model answer helps more than "It's." alone.
- C: seg8 ends on "is" and "It's", so the voice drops. seg20 has a "?" in the middle of a sentence. "hoo, like an owl" reads fine.
- Decision: seg8 -> "Answer, My hero is my coach. Or, It's pizza." (A+C). seg20 quote both questions (A+C).

#### L352 When Is the Concert?
- A: The concert dialogue and Birimo's "When is lunch? Where is lunch?" are natural. seg8 "Answer both with It's in." ends on a preposition. In seg9, "Round lips:" has no article.
- B: when = time, where = place is clear. The wh/th contrast and the tip are correct. Model answers (December / the gym) repeat the target language.
- C: "It's in." sounds cut off. The colon in "Round lips: when, where." cannot be voiced. seg20 has the same mid-sentence "?" as L351.
- Decision: seg8 -> "Answer, It's in December. Or, It's in the gym." (A+C). seg9 -> "Round your lips. When, where." and the JA くちを まるく して。(A+C). seg20 quote the questions (A+C).

#### L353 Why Do You Like It?
- A: "Brrr!", "Because I can ski and play in the snow!" and Birimo's bread line are natural. The aiTask "Why do you like your favorite season?" is odd because no season is named yet.
- B: why -> because and how -> a way are clear. The rhyme pairs (why/my, how/now) are correct. The aiTask should ask which season first, then why. In seg9, "Open your mouth wide, then make it round" fits how better than why (single flag, kept).
- C: seg20 "You can ask Why do you like winter? and answer with because." has a mid-sentence "?".
- Decision: aiTask starterEn -> "...What season do you like? Why?" (A+B). seg20 quote the question and the words "because" / "how" (A+C).

#### L354 Interview Contest
- A: The final-round dialogue is natural ("I want to meet a real astronaut!"). seg8 "Ask, listen, then ask one more." is a good interview tip.
- B: In seg9, "The others start with a soft w sound, and how starts with h." contradicts itself, because "the others" includes how. In seg7, Birimo answers and then asks a question, and Riko answers, all with no speaker names. A child cannot follow who is talking.
- C: seg7 needs "Birimo says," / "Riko says," to change voices. seg9 is long and the order is confusing. seg6 alternates Kiko/Haru clearly, so it is OK.
- Decision: seg7 add "Birimo says," and "Riko says," plus the JA speaker names (B+C). seg9 -> "Who sounds like hoo. How starts with h. The others start with a soft w sound." and the JA (B+C).

#### L355 Question Master
- A: The reports are natural ("She likes music because it makes her happy."). In the conversation, Kiko's "You're welcome! It was fun." comes after Haru's report, so it answers nobody. The seg7 narration already puts Kiko first.
- B: The she/he point for reporting is clear. The conversation order should match the teacher's retelling. "You are all question masters!" is uncontracted for emphasis, which is OK.
- C: seg20 "you can say Thank you for the interview." needs a pause and quote. "Why says wye." reads fine. "I ask, Let's report..." is slightly odd but ends with a question (single flag).
- Decision: swap conversation lines 2 and 3 (speaker/en/ja) (A+B). seg20 quote the phrase (A+C).

#### L356 Welcome to the Party!
- A: The party prep is warm and natural ("Sure! I can do it.", "Ding-dong!" at the door is right). The reported speech `Kiko said, "I want to sing at the party!"` is correct. seg6 retells it as "Riko tells us Kiko wants to sing", which is fine.
- B: The three steps (welcome, name, something you like) are clear. In seg9, "thay" is a made-up spelling. "They rhymes with day" teaches the sound with a known word.
- C: TTS will mispronounce "thay". "with your tongue out" tacked on at the end is awkward.
- Decision: seg9 -> "They rhymes with day. Tongue out a little!" and the JA (A+C).

#### L357 I Can Speak English!
- A: The cake scene is natural and warm ("I want the biggest piece!"). In seg1, "I can. I want to. Let's!" are fragments. seg8 "I can is for things you can do now." reads as broken grammar without quotes.
- B: The can / want to / Let's contrast is a good review. In seg9, "Magic e makes a say its name": the letter a and the article a look the same.
- C: TTS reads "a" as /ə/, so "the letter a" is needed. The seg1 fragments and the seg20 comma chain ("You can say I can speak English, and I want...") are hard to voice.
- Decision: seg1 -> `Use "I can," "I want to," and "Let's."` (A+C). seg8 quote the three phrases (A+C). seg9 -> "the letter a" (B+C). seg20 quote the sentences (A+C).

#### L358 Memories of the Year
- A: The album talk is warm ("My best memory is all of you!"). In seg1, "My best memory is. We went to. I saw. It was." is a formula read aloud. In seg19, "Go in the past is went." is not how a teacher says it.
- B: The go/went, see/saw, is/was point is clear. "You can make it up!" is good for shy children. The model sentences in seg1 repeat this lesson's own lines. The JA ポイント matches.
- C: The seg1 fragments end on function words. seg20 is a comma chain. "Corn, or." reads fine.
- Decision: seg1 -> the four model sentences, and the JA (A+B+C). seg19 -> "The past of go is went." (A+B). seg20 quote the sentences (A+C).

#### L359 Grand Finale Review
- A: "Hey, party people!" and the question game are natural. "the most big" (seg9) is wrong English. seg8 "Answer, I learned. Why? Answer, because." sounds like full sentences. The TPR item "Jump the highest." is a little odd (better "Jump as high as you can!"), but it is an item tied to the -est focus (single flag, kept).
- B: "the most big" models a wrong form, so it is an objective fix. "est means the most" matches いちばん. In seg17, "I learned... and Because..." has a capital in the middle of a sentence.
- C: seg8 needs "..." pauses. "How do you say pan in English?": TTS reads "pan" as the English word /pæn/, not パン (noted, kept: it is a conversation item and the joke still works).
- Decision: seg8 -> "Answer, I learned... Why? Answer, Because..." (A+C). seg9 -> "Biggest, est means the most." and the JA (objective). seg17 -> "I learned... Because..." (A+C).

#### L360 World 12 Grand Finale
- A: The finale lines are warm and natural ("I'm so proud of you.", "See you in junior high school!", the new-map teaser). In seg8, "I am, I can, my best memory is, and thank you." is a chain. In seg18, "You are Kingdom of English champions!" is a noun stack. In seg19, "tell who you are" can mean "recognize". The aiTask "Please tell me about your year!" is a please-command.
- B: A model speech in seg8 shows how the sentences link (the subtitleJa keeps the formula, as in earlier blocks). "say who you are" matches じぶんの こと. "Can you tell me...?" is the friendlier form from the style guide.
- C: The seg8 chain cannot be read naturally. In seg11, "Now party moves." has no verb or pause. The seg18 noun stack is hard to say.
- Decision: seg8 -> "I am Haru. I can speak English now. My best memory is camp. Thank you!" (A+B+C). seg11 -> "Now for party moves!" (A+C). seg18 -> "You're champions of the Kingdom of English!" (A+C). seg19 "tell" -> "say" (A+B). aiTask starterEn -> "Can you tell me about your year?" (A+B).
