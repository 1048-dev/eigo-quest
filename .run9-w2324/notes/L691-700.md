### Run 9 review notes: L691-700 (world 24, Stage 3)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, "A. Or b.", OK, US praise, Ding-dong). There are 24 patches in 7 lessons. L691, L694 and L697 have no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. The station and ticket English is real US usage ("One way or round trip?", "How can I help you?", "Is this the right platform for ~?"). No present perfect appears before L696 (tested), and the present perfect explanations are correct (have + third form, ever/never, "When did you go?" in the past simple, once/twice/three times). Themes: (1) **US pronunciation of "been"**: L696 taught "Seen and been rhyme. Een!" and the tipJa said both are イーン. In American English "been" is /bɪn/ (like "bin"). (2) **AI starters that break the story**: in L693 the clerk answered a request the child never made ("A ticket to Rainbow Falls? Sure."), and in L699 Kiko said "I've ridden this train twenty times!" although the friends are on it for the first time. (3) **L700 seg13** repeated seg12 word for word instead of the speed challenge its slide shows. (4) Small TTS and wording points: "One, like gone." (TTS says the number), a "?" at the end of a statement, "two same parts", "a small food", "a happy answer", "with a clear voice". Every new sentence has 15 words or fewer. The check script passes with ALL OK. Touched lessons are 213-228 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### Block-wide notes
- **"been" pronunciation**: US "been" is /bɪn/. Please grep worlds 24+ for claims that seen/been rhyme or that been is イーン (L696 fixed). L700 sounds[0] "/iːn/" is only for seen (fine). Later review lessons (L705, L719 "Be, was, been") are fine as long as they do not say "een".
- **Speed-challenge segments that copy the TPR segment**: L700 seg13 was a copy of seg12. A quick check: seg13.say should start with "Speed challenge!" in every lesson.
- **Spoken suffixes that are real words**: "One, like gone" (L700) is read as the number. Watch for "-one", "-own" etc. spoken alone in say.
- **Stress spell-outs with capital syllables** ("CAN, a, da. A, MER, i, ca. IT, a, ly.", L691-695, L705, L719): house style since world 23. Sentence-initial "A," and "IT" may be read as letter names by some TTS voices. Kept (single flag); if the TTS check shows letter names, lowercase the first unstressed syllable ("a, MER, i, ca" mid-sentence) or say the whole word first.
- **teacherScript "Or, B"**: L696, L698, L699 (and L718) teacherScripts still have "Or, No, ..." / "Or, I've never ...". The mechanical pass did not touch teacherScript. Harmless for TTS, kept; fix centrally if you want the same style as say.
- **AI starters that assume something**: L693 (answered a request the child never made) and L699 (partner claims many rides on a train the group just boarded). Worth checking starters for invented history that contradicts the story.
- Kept on purpose: L691 tpr[3] "Jump on the train!" and seg8 "Above means higher."; L692 "Great shopping!" / "Super math!" and yen for the cloud bun; L693 "Five, one-way, tickets!" and the tested Japanese hint; L695 bowing TPR; L696 seg2 "Last year, yesterday, any time!" and "After have, use seen."; L697 Ms. Emma praising Kiko for both questions; L699 "Today, Conductor Nimbus showed us..." and tipJa ドローン; L700 two Haru lines in a row (he answers, then asks).
