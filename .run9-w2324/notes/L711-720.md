#### L711 How Long Does It Take?
- A: "How long does it take?" / "It takes two hours." and the route map are natural. "It takes about thirty minutes by underground." is British. An American says "by subway". In seg4, "Brushing your teeth takes three minutes." The usual advice to US kids is two minutes.
- B: Same flag on "by underground" (US standard). The script line "Take here means use time." is ungrammatical. seg9 "Polite means kind." is wrong (polite = good manners), and the JA says ていねい, so EN and JA do not match. The im- before p rule is clear. The tip's "p で はじまる ことばの はんたいは im-" is simplified but true for the words taught.
- C: "Take here means use time." reads as the command "Take here!" "Tick, tock. Take!" is fun. "The h is quiet." is fine for kids.
- Decision: "by underground" -> "by subway" in expressions[2], aiTask target, recap, seg1 slide, seg8, seg16 and the teacherScript (route map line and the answer) (A+B). teacherScript "Take here means use time." -> "Here, take means it needs time." (A+B). seg4 three -> two minutes, JA 2ふん (objective). seg9 "Polite means kind." -> "Polite means you have good manners." (objective).
