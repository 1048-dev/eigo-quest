#### L726 It Might Be a Bat
- A: The sounds in the dark passage and "It might be a bat / the wind" are natural. In Luma's guide, "She flies around at night, so don't worry" is not a reason (flying at night is not why you should not worry).
- B: Same flag. "Might means maybe. Use it when you're not sure" is the right rule, and "Hand down low" starts the hand meter. seg9 "Say iv, short." is an invented respelling.
- C: "iv" may be read as the Roman numeral IV or "I.V.". The real word "give" has the same /ɪv/ sound.
- Decision: teacherScript "She flies around at night, so don't worry." -> "She flies around at night. Don't worry." (A+B). seg9 (say, subtitleEn) "Say iv, short." -> "It sounds like give." with JA give と おなじ おと。 (B+C).
