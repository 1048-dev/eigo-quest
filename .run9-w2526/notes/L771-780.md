- B: Same two flags. The JA of seg7 already says もっと おしえて, so "Tell me more!" matches it better. "We need ~ / What else do we need?" is clear. seg7 paraphrases Birimo's line ("What else do we need? My dragon wings!") instead of the conversation line; the slide shows the real line (kept, the meaning matches).
