# Legal document drafts (Japanese)
Write drafts for the owner to have checked by a professional later: docs/legal/terms.md (利用規約), docs/legal/privacy.md (プライバシーポリシー),
docs/legal/tokushoho.md (特定商取引法に基づく表記, with placeholders for operator name/address/phone/price).
Base them on what the app actually does (parent accounts, children data, AI conversation sent to an AI API with redaction, LINE delivery, email, cookies, storage, deletion).
Mark every place that needs the owner's real information or a professional's judgment with 【要確認】. Add simple pages /terms, /privacy, /tokushoho that render these files,
and links in the footer and registration screen. Never invent the operator's real details.
