### Run 9 review notes: L451-460 (world 16, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "Yes, I did. Or no, I didn't." says, OK, thumbs-up, US praise, Ding-dong, "Last time" in L451, "speed challenge", lowercase after "Now, / Then,") or the block conventions (the quiz "blank" placeholder, letter names like "E D", Japanese in `say` ending with "."). There are 51 patches in 9 lessons. L453 has no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The most common fix is objective Japanese: "sing の むかしは sang" / "とくべつな むかしの ことば" uses むかし ("long ago") to mean "past form", in L456-L460 seg1 and L456 seg20. I changed it to "sing は sang" and すぎた かたち, the block term. Other fixes: "tell your weekend" / "Tell me your diary" (unidiomatic, L455 and L460), clipped or non-English teacher lines ("so is", "Answer for you", "Just add.", "Now, I know!", "Shut!"), "a plains pancake" (L452), a tense and "either" error in the L458 riddle chat, and Ms. Emma calling herself "Ms. Emma" in the L459 script. Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 217-226 s long with 97-99% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L451 The Old Ruins
- A: The arrival at the ruins is natural ("Look! Stone walls and a tall tower!", "Maybe a thousand years ago."). In seg8, "That's the past, so lived. ... That's true now, so is." is clipped. A teacher would say "so we say lived / so we say is".
- B: The e + d rule and the /d/ vs /t/ ending are correct (lived, smiled /d/; liked, danced /t/). The past vs present contrast in seg8 is a good point. In seg17 subtitleJa, "き の うえの いえ" has a broken space.
- C: "That's true now, so is." sounds like "so is (she)" = "me too" when read aloud. The rest reads well ("Swish, swish!", "Look up, way up to the top!").
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "so we say lived" / "so we say is" (A+C). seg17 subtitleJa き の うえ -> きの うえ (objective, JA).

- Decision: conversation.lines[2] en/ja, seg6 say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa/slide item -> "I tried a pancake on the plains." (A+B).

#### L456 We Sang by the Fire

#### L458 Birimo Knew the Answer

- C: "Now, I know!" has the wrong ring for the eureka moment. The riddle reads well. "Rumble, rumble." is good.
- Decision: conversation.lines[2] en/ja, seg6 say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa/slide item -> "Hmm. I don't know the answer." / うーん。こたえが わからないなあ。 (A+B). seg3 say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa -> "Someone asks a question, and you give the answer." (A+B). seg11 say/subtitleEn "Now, I know!" -> "Oh, I know!" (A+C). seg1 say/subtitleJa "know の むかしは" -> "know は" (objective). phonics.tipJa "swimed" vs seg9 "swimmed" (two different wrong forms) is kept.
