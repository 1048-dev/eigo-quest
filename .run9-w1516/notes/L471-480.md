### Run 9 review notes: L471-480 (world 16, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "Yes, I did. Or no, I didn't." says, OK and "Are you OK now?" in L473, thumbs-up, US praise, Ding-dong, "Last time" in L472, "speed challenge", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First,"). There are 46 patches in 9 lessons (L477 has no change). There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The main themes: Ms. Emma narrating herself in the third person ("Ms. Emma says" in L475/L476, while L479 already says "I ask"); unidiomatic phrases ("keep a talk going", "tell your best day", "Trouble story", "Dolphins are happy news", "Thank the places you visit"); the Japanese "むかし" used as a name for the past form (L471, L474, L476), where this world otherwise says すぎた かたち; one grammar error ("O and a quiet E says"); one UK word ("bin"); and "Show time!" -> "Showtime!" (US spelling, same as L180/L405). Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. Shell tools were not available in this run, so I could not run check.ts. I checked by hand that every `before` string is in world-16.json, that the new slide item (L479 seg6) matches conversation.lines[3], and that L478, the lesson that grows most (+8 words), stays at about 225 s by the timing.ts formula. The coordinator should run check.ts before applying.

#### L471 Summer Stories
- A: The traveler role-play ("Let's pretend. I'm a traveler named Leo.") and the beach and fireworks lines are natural. In seg18, "Summer is over, so it's was." is not how a teacher says it. seg8 already has the natural form, "so we say was".
- B: "It was ～ + one more sentence + How about you?" is a clear three-step frame, and the three -ed sounds are introduced with good examples. In seg8 (say and subtitleJa), "was は is の むかし（の かたち）" names the past form むかし ("long ago"), which is unnatural and does not match すぎた かたち, the term this world uses everywhere (titles, slides, L476 to L480).
- C: seg18 "so it's was" stumbles (it's / was clash). "Want, wan-ted!" and the clapped extra beat read well. "E D" and "A soft d" are the block's letter spell-out pattern (kept).
- Decision: seg18 say/subtitleEn -> "Summer is over, so we say was." (A+C). seg8 say/subtitleJa "むかし（の かたち）" -> "すぎた かたち" (objective, JA).

#### L472 Who Did You Go With?
- A: The trip chat is natural ("What was the best part?", "We stayed up late and looked at the stars!"). seg19 "Now you can keep a talk going." is not the idiom. The usual phrase is "keep the conversation going". "In summer, I visited..." (instead of "This summer") is a little loose, but OK (single flag).
- B: The follow-up questions are a good next step after L471. In seg8, "did の あとは ed なし! Go, not went." uses a rule about ed to explain went, which has no ed. The real rule is the base form (もとの かたち). The same "ed なし" shorthand is fine in L475, where the example is learned -> learn.
- C: Reads well ("Smile, click!", "Bye-bye, sea!"). "Teeth on your lip for the F" could be "top teeth", but it is clear enough (single flag).
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleJa "did の あとは ed なし" -> "did の あとは もとの かたち" (A+B). seg19 say/subtitleEn -> "Now you can keep the conversation going." (A+B).

#### L473 Sounds Fun! Oh No!
- A: The ice-cream story and Birimo's "Sounds fun! Well, not the first part." are funny and natural. The salt-in-tea opener is good. "Trouble story?" (seg1) and "trouble stories" (seg20) are not natural English. In seg19, "Dolphins are happy news." is illogical, because the swim is the news, not the dolphins.
- B: Same flag on "trouble story". seg8 and seg12 already use "Good news? / Bad news?", so seg1 and seg20 should use the same pair for one clear contrast. Same flag on seg19. "Are you OK now? ― Yes, I'm fine." as a later check-in is well placed.
- C: "Trouble story?" is clunky to say. The rest reads well ("Eyes wide! Oh!", "Oops, I dropped it!").
- Decision: seg1 "Happy story? ... Trouble story?" -> "Good news? ... Bad news?" in say/subtitleEn, with JA いい ニュース / わるい ニュース (A+B). seg20 "to happy stories ... to trouble stories" -> "to good news ... to bad news", with JA to match (A+B). seg19 -> "That's happy news.", JA それは うれしい ニュースだね (A+B). The teacherScript ("For a happy story / For a bad story") and testPoints JA are kept.

#### L474 My Best Day Ever

#### L475 Memory Interview
- A: The TV studio frame is natural ("Lights, camera, action!", "You're a pro interviewer!"). In seg6, Ms. Emma reads "Ms. Emma says, Riko, you're the interviewer." Teachers do not narrate themselves in the third person, and L479 seg6 already uses "I ask".
- B: The When / How was it pair is clear, and "after did, no E D. Learn, not learned." is correct here. phonics.tipJa uses the kanji 重ねる, which is above level, while the seg9 subtitle already writes かさねる.
- C: "Ms. Emma says" in Ms. Emma's own voice sounds odd. "Ask, asked. Just add it." is a little vague, but OK (single flag). "Uh-huh, uh-huh. Scribble, scribble!" reads well.
- Decision: seg6 say/subtitleEn "Ms. Emma says," -> "I say," with JA エマ先生「」 -> わたし「」 (A+C). phonics.tipJa 重ねる -> かさねる (objective, JA).

#### L476 Past Tense Quest
- A: The treasure-box password game is natural ("Click! The box is open! We did it!", "Ugh, it's heavy!"). seg6 has the same self-narration problem: "Ms. Emma says, Wait." "What's the past of buy?" is ordinary teacher talk (also in reviewed W12), so it is kept.
- B: The irregular forms come in small sets, and "Not goed. Never!" is a good contrast. seg8 "go の むかしは went" has the same むかし problem. In seg9, "O and a quiet E says oh." has a plural subject, so the verb should be say.
- C: Same flag on "Ms. Emma says". "goed", "eated" and "buyed" are intentional wrong forms (kept). The spell-outs "O U G H T" and "E W" are the block pattern.
- Decision: seg6 say/subtitleEn "Ms. Emma says," -> "I say," with JA わたし「」 (A+C). seg8 say/subtitleJa "go の むかしは" -> "go の すぎた かたちは" (objective, JA). seg9 say/subtitleEn "says oh" -> "say oh" (objective, grammar).

#### L479 Ruins of Memory Review

The script tip "Thank the places you visit" is not natural English. "Into the bin!" is British, and US English says "trash can".
- B: Same flag on "danced in the picture". The JA おどってたね already means "were dancing". "Thank the places" is abstract for kids, and "Take care of the places" fits the trash TPR. The What / Where / Did you contrast and the "Yes, I liked!" quiz are correct.
- C: "Did you? gets yes or no." is terse, but readable (single flag). Spell-outs are the block pattern.
- Decision: conversation.lines[3], seg6 say/subtitleEn and seg6 slide.items[2] -> "I went to the wall painting. The people in the picture were dancing." (A+B). teacherScript "Thank the places you visit" -> "Take care of the places you visit" (A+B). seg4 "Into the bin!" -> "Into the trash can!" (objective, US usage).
