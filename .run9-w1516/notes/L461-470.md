### Run 9 review notes: L461-470 (world 16, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, OK, thumbs-up, US praise, Ding-dong, "Last time", "speed challenge", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First,") or the central decisions (Well done!, Japanese in `say` ending with ".", letter spell-outs like "E W" and "O U G H T", TPR capitals after "And,"). The L467 "First, ... Then, ... After that, ..." story pattern is kept as intended. There are 25 patches in 9 lessons (L468 has no change). There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The most common fix is again "tell ~" used for "talk about" (L462 "tell a funny mistake", L467 "Tell your morning"). Others: a wrong grammar rule in L462 ("for was, ask with were"), a false cross-reference in the L467 script (World 14 cooking taught "First / Next", not "then / after that"), double-word stumbles read aloud ("Then and, and the last one" in L465, "Then use first, then" in L470), a pronoun with no referent in L466 seg6, a spoken quiz blank "hmm" in L464, and small EN/JA or frame mismatches (L465 goalJa, L469 Haru's line). Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 208-228 s long with 97-99% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L461 I Bought a Map
- A: The souvenir shop is natural ("Take your time, Haru.", "It was only two hundred yen."). "Here we are, the souvenir shop." reads as a fragment. A teacher says "Here we are at the souvenir shop." The quiz "Yesterday at the shop..." is story time, not a reference to the previous lesson, so it is right to keep it.
- B: buy -> bought and "did + buy" (seg8) are clear, and the o_e magic-e link (write -> wrote) is well explained. "buy の むかしは bought" uses the block's むかし wording (kept). EN and JA match.
- C: Reads well. The "Here we are, the souvenir shop" comma makes a flat, odd pause. "The I changes to O" is fine for TTS, because the letter name and the pronoun sound the same.
- Decision: conversation.lines[0].en -> "Here we are at the souvenir shop. You can buy one thing each." (A+C).

#### L462 I Thought It Was a Ghost!
- A: The sheet "ghost" is fun and natural ("Eek! Is it a ghost?", "Phew!"). In seg8, "And for was, ask with were." is a wrong rule. The script itself asks "Was it a ghost?" Were is used because of you. In seg20, "You can tell a funny mistake" is unidiomatic.
- B: Same flag on seg8, because it teaches a false rule right when was/were questions are new (the JA was の しつもんは were has the same error). Same flag on "tell a funny mistake". "A spooky friend in stories" vs JA ふしぎな ともだち is a mild mismatch (single flag, kept).
- C: Reads well ("Shh, shh.", "One, two, three, whoosh!"). "Oh, oh, oh!" is fine.
- Decision: seg8 say/subtitleEn -> "And with you, use were.", JA -> you の ときは were を つかうよ (A+B). seg20 "tell a funny mistake" -> "talk about a funny mistake" (A+B).

#### L464 It Wasn't Easy

#### L467 First, Then, After That
- A: The seed story and "It's like a recipe!" are natural. The script tip "Remember the cooking class in World 14? First, then, after that." is false. World 14 (L401) taught "First, ~. Next, ~." In seg17, "Tell your morning in order" is unidiomatic. In seg12, "After that, Plant" and "Finally, Water" keep TPR capitals mid-sentence, while seg11 already uses lowercase "First, open / Then, look".
- B: Same flag on "Tell your morning". The aught/ought "same sound" point is clear. Keeping the "First, ... Then, ..." commas is intended.
- C: "Aw-t." may be read as "aw tee" by TTS (single flag, kept, reported). Otherwise reads well ("Green thumbs!").
- Decision: teacherScript tip -> "Remember the cooking class in World 14? We said First and Next. Stories use order words, too!" (objective, fact). seg17 "Tell your morning" -> "Talk about your morning" (A+B). seg12 -> "After that, plant a seed. ... Finally, water the plant." (objective, matches the block's lowercase rule).
