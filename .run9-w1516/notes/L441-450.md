### Run 9 review notes: L441-450 (world 15, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "Yes, I am! Or no, I'm not." says, OK, thumbs-up, US praise, L445 "That's so nice", Ding-dong, "Last time", "speed challenge", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First,"). There are 35 patches in 9 lessons. L447 has no change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The English in this block is already clean and child-friendly, so most fixes are small. Themes: (1) "Let's say it." with no model sentence after it in the teacherScript (L441, L443); (2) odd wording a teacher would not say ("It's evening in the tent", "Turn it over" for a page, "kite ride", "tell every day from right now", "Tell about your picture"); (3) TTS and reading problems (a bare heteronym "Live.", "And now. Freeze!", a stacked-comma recap line); (4) a fact slip ("Sunflowers always face the sun"); (5) unnatural Japanese (くうきに かく, かく ひとたち, かきてたち); (6) one capital after "Finally," that the block fix did not cover. Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 216-227 s long with 97-98% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L441 A Rainy Afternoon
- A: The rainy tent scene is warm and natural ("Drip, drop", "Here, Birimo. Sit next to me. It's dry here."). In the script, "Let's say it." is followed by nothing, and the next line is "OK, everyone, ...". The quiz "It's raining, so which is right?" makes the weather sound like the reason for the grammar. "Today is rainy, but tomorrow may be sunny." is fine ("might" is a little more natural, but this is a single flag).
- B: The y = ee rule for longer words is clear and matches the tipJa. Same flag on the quiz "so". Haru's "We're playing cards." vs JA トランプを しよう is close enough, because it reads as "that's what we're doing" (single flag, kept).
- C: The script stops dead after "Let's say it." The quiz line reads fine aloud. "i n g" spell-outs follow the central decision.
- Decision: teacherScript "Let's say it." + "Listen! The rain is getting stronger." (A+C). seg18 say/subtitleEn "It's raining, so which is right?" -> "It's raining. Which is right?" (A+B). The JA already has two sentences.

#### L442 Rainbow Over the Plains
- A: The after-rain scene is natural ("Shake off the rain. Like a puppy!", "Why is there a rainbow? Is it magic?"). "Coming out means appearing." uses a harder word, but it is OK as a gloss. "Rain water" is written as two words.
- B: The short-word y = eye rule and the "Last time, we learned rainy" link are good. The JA くうきに にじを かいて (tpr, seg11, seg13) is unnatural. Japanese says くうちゅうに かく.
- C: Reads well. "F, L, Y." is a clean letter spell-out. "Why do birds fly in the dry sky?" is a deliberate sound sentence and is easy to say.
- Decision: seg4 "Rain water" -> "Rainwater" (objective, spelling). tpr[1].ja and the seg11/seg13 subtitleJa くうきに -> くうちゅうに (objective, JA).

#### L443 Sunflower Field

- C: Same gap after "Let's say it." "Tongue up high, then slide. Yuh!" is readable.

#### L445 Plains Diary
- A: The diary-sharing scene is natural, and Haru's cookie joke works. seg0 "It's evening in the tent." is odd. seg5 "Page. Turn it over." means flipping something upside down, but you turn a page. "Close your notebook. Flap!" is a wing sound.
- B: Same flags on seg0 and seg5. The JA こんばんは、かく ひとたち！ (seg0) and すごい かきてたち！ (seg13) are not natural Japanese for "writers". さっか is the natural word. The privacy note (no real name or address) is good.
- C: The recap "start a diary with Today, I'm, add the weather, and give a reason with because" stacks commas around a quoted fragment and cannot be read clearly. "Flap!" sounds wrong for a notebook. "Snap!" works.
- Decision: seg0 -> "It's evening, and we're in the tent." with JA さっかの みんな！ ゆうがた、テントの なかに いるよ。 (A+B). seg5 -> "Turn the page." (A+B). seg12 "Flap!" -> "Snap!" (A+C). seg13 subtitleJa かきてたち -> さっかたち (objective, JA). seg20 -> "You can start a diary with Today, I'm. Then add the weather and a reason with because." (A+C).

#### L449 Windy Plains Review
- A: The goodbye picnic and the wind feather are a warm ending. seg12 "One more kite ride!" is wrong, because nobody rides a kite (JA もう いちど たこあげ). The recap "tell every day from right now" uses the "tell A from B" idiom, which kids will not get and which sounds like "say every day". TPR "Blow it up high." can also be heard as "blow it up" (explode). It is a learning item and is clear in context, so it is not an objective error (single flag, kept, reported).
- B: Same flag on the recap idiom. The habit-vs-now quiz is correct. The seg13 slide title "Let's move!" breaks the "Speed challenge" title used in every other lesson.
- C: "One more kite ride!" is fine to say but means the wrong thing. "Wind masters!" is fine.
- Decision: seg12 -> "One more time with the kite!" (A+B). seg13 slide.title -> "Speed challenge" (objective, consistency). seg20 -> "You can ask what people are doing. And you can tell every day and right now apart." (A+B).

#### L450 World 15 Grand Review
- A: The live show, Birimo's runaway kite and the ruins cliffhanger work well. "Next is World 16, Ruins of Memory!" matches the L451 opening.
- B: The three-step report (Look! / who is doing what / Keep going!) is clear, and is/are for one or two people is a good final check. The vocab "live" (adjective, /laɪv/) can be confused with the verb live.
- C: In seg4, the bare "Live." at the start and the end is a heteronym. TTS will likely read it as /lɪv/. Putting it in the phrase "live show" makes the right sound more likely.
- Decision: seg4 say/subtitleEn -> "Live. Like a live show! It's happening right now, at this moment. ... Live show!", JA ライブショーの live だよ！ ... live show！ (B+C). The vocab item "live" is unchanged.

- **"Tell about ~"**: fixed in L448 seg1. It still appears in other blocks' goal lines.
