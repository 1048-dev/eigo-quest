### Run 9 review notes: L421-430 (world 15, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, "Yes, I am! Or no, I'm not." says, OK, thumbs-up, US praise, Ding-dong, "Last time", "speed challenge", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First,") or the central decisions (Japanese in `say`, letter spell-outs like "C E", "T T, P P", "i-n-g"). There are 39 patches in 10 lessons (every lesson has at least one change). There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. Main themes: a UK phrase ("Run on the spot" -> "Run in place", L422/L423); wrap-up sentences over the 12-word limit, or with a dangling second clause (L421, L423, L430); unclear teacher instructions ("Swap them!", "I'll ask, and you do it. What are you doing? Pull the string!", "Answer me with the truth"); odd collocations ("Yummy work", "in the town", "call her on a video call", "bird house"); and the TTS non-word "Baa-rilliant" (a pun on UK "brilliant"). Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. All ten lessons are 219-228 s long with 97-98% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L421 The Wind Is Blowing!
- A: The arrival on the plains and the hat scene are natural ("I've got it! Here you go, Haru."). "Right now needs is plus ing." (seg19) is not something a teacher would say. The seg20 wrap-up is one 20-word sentence, and "and add ing" loses its subject.
- B: The quiet-e rule is introduced clearly, and the quiz contrast "The wind blowing / The wind is blowing" fits. Same flag on the seg20 length (over the Stage 2 limit). "Let the ing ring in your nose" is a fine cue for the nasal /ŋ/.
- C: In seg9, "Make, wave, smile, shine end with a quiet E" sounds like four commands until the verb arrives. Seg19 "Right now needs" trips. The seg20 sentence has no place to breathe.
- Decision: seg9 -> "Make, wave, smile, and shine end with a quiet E." (A+C). seg19 -> "For right now, use is plus ing." (A+C). seg20 split into "You can say the wind is blowing and the grass is moving. And you can add ing to make and smile." (A+C). The subtitleJa lines already match and are kept.

#### L422 What Are You Doing?
- A: The kite scene and Birimo flying off with the string are fun and natural. "Run on the spot" (seg12) is British (US: "run in place"). In seg13, "I'll ask, and you do it. What are you doing? Pull the string!" does not work: a question is followed by commands.
- B: Same flag on seg13: a child hears "What are you doing?" and then has to act, not answer, which confuses the target question. The ride/hide/slide/write set is a good second step for the quiet-e rule.
- C: Reads well ("Wheee, I'm sliding down the hill!"). "Hold it tight, or your kite flies away!" is fine aloud (single note, kept).
- Decision: seg12 "Run on the spot" -> "Run in place" (objective, US). seg13 -> "I'll say it, and you do it.", with "What are you doing?" dropped from say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa (A+B).

#### L423 Look! She's Running!
- A: The race commentary is natural ("On your mark, get set, go!", "Kiko wins! Haru is second, and the rabbit is third."). Seg11 also has "Run on the spot". Seg20 runs "and you can cheer, You can do it!" into one 16-word sentence.
- B: The she's/he's contrast and the Haru quiz are clear. "Look! He's jumping. Look! She's dancing." for a friend in a race is playful but OK (single flag, kept).
- C: The seg20 run-on "you can cheer, You can do it!" blurs where the quote starts. Everything else reads well.
- Decision: seg11 "Run on the spot" -> "Run in place" (objective, US). seg20 split: "You can say she's running and he's running. And you can cheer, You can do it!" (A+C).

#### L424 Are You Having Fun?
- A: The picnic and the sandwich joke are natural ("No, I'm not! I'm just smelling it."). "Answer me with the truth." (seg17) is not idiomatic. "Yummy work today!" (seg20) is an odd collocation, and its JA おいしい がんばり is odd too.
- B: In seg8, "You are having fun. Swap them!" does not say what to swap, and that is the core point of the lesson. Same flag on seg17. The short-answer point is clear.
- C: Seg17 "Answer me with the truth" is clunky to say, and "Yummy work" lands flat. "Bye-bye, E, hello, ing!" is fun aloud. "the wind is soft today" in the script is a little unusual (single flag, kept).
- Decision: seg8 "Swap them!" -> "Swap you and are!", JA you と are を いれかえて！ (A+B). seg17 -> "Be honest!" (A+B+C). The JA ほんとうの きもちで こたえてね still fits. seg20 -> "What a yummy picnic!", JA おいしい ピクニックだったね！ (A+C).

#### L425 Windy Day Video Call
- A: The video call is natural ("That one looks like a horse!", "I'm drinking tea in a cafe."). The script "Today Ms. Emma is in the town" should be "in town", and "Let's call her on a video call" is redundant.
- B: The I'm/we're contrast and the skate/jump comparison are well staged. The privacy tip is good. EN and JA match.
- C: The script's "call her on a video call" repeats call. Segments read well ("Ring, ring!", "Crystal clear, like a great video call!").
- Decision: teacherScript "in the town" -> "in town", "Let's call her on a video call." -> "Let's give her a video call." (A+C).

#### L426 Horses Are Eating Grass
- A: The farm scene is warm ("Aww, so cute!", "Here you are, horses!"). "Baa-rilliant!" (seg19) puns on "brilliant", which the block replaced as UK praise. "Fun fact, one sheep..." wants a colon.
- B: The doubling rule and the sheep singular/plural fact are clear and well timed. "They're trotting slowly." with JA あるいて is loose (trotting is faster than walking), but it is a learning item and fine for kids (kept).
- C: TTS will read "Baa-rilliant" badly. "T T, P P, T T, P P." is the block spell-out pattern (kept).
- Decision: seg19 -> "Fun fact: one sheep, two sheep, the word doesn't change. Baa! Great job!", JA メェー！ よく できました！ (A+C).

#### L427 The Rabbit Is Behind the Rock
- A: The hide-and-seek scene is natural ("Ready or not, here I come!", "I found you, Mr. Fox!"). The script's "let's be quiet like hunters" is the wrong image for friendly animal watching, and seg0 already calls the kids "detectives".
- B: Same flag on "hunters" (a fox is already chasing the rabbit, so a hunting image is not warm). The hand gestures for behind / in front of / next to are good. In seg7, "Oh, good." has no speaker (it is Riko's line), but it reads fine (single flag, kept).
- C: Reads well ("Peekaboo!", "Super sneaky!").
- Decision: teacherScript "quiet like hunters" -> "quiet like detectives" (A+B).

#### L428 Between the Trees
- A: The bird-watching scene is natural ("Which trees? There are so many."). "A cozy little bird house" (seg4) sounds like a birdhouse, the wooden box people build. In seg20, "You can say between, and between this and that." is confusing.
- B: Same flag on "bird house" (a wrong picture for nest). "Between needs two things, one on each side" is clear. In seg7, "It's in its nest. Look, three eggs!" has no "I say" attribution (single flag, kept).
- C: The seg20 "between, and between this and that" stumbles. "One time, carefully!" is fine.
- Decision: seg4 -> "A cozy little bird home made of sticks." (A+B). seg20 -> "You can say between the big tree and the small tree.", with subtitleJa to match (A+C).

#### L429 What Is the Dog Doing?
- A: The farm dog scene is natural ("Can I pet your dog? Always ask first, just like Riko!"). "Pawsome work!" is a common kid pun and OK.
- B: The it's/is-it point and the "Yes, it is / Yes, it does" quiz fit well. The JA in seg3 has すきま (a gap or crack) for a dug space in the ground. That is the wrong word.

#### L430 Animal Watching Report
- A: The report day and Birimo's cookie report are fun and natural. "Any animals are OK." (seg17) is unnatural. The seg20 wrap-up is a 15-word sentence. Seg13 is titled "Let's move!" but it is the speed challenge.
- B: The three-rule ing review is clear. Same flag on seg20 length. The aiTask "What animals did you watch?" could be "see", but "watch" ties to animal watching (single flag, kept).
- C: Seg17 "Any animals are OK" is clunky aloud. "Ing, ing, ing!" may be read oddly by TTS, but it is a short chant (single flag, kept).
- Decision: seg13 slide.title "Let's move!" -> "Speed challenge" (objective, consistency). seg17 -> "Any animal is OK." (A+C). seg20 split: "You can say what animals are doing and where. And you know three ing rules." (A+C).
