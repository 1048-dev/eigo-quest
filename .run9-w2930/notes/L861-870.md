- Decision: teacherScript and seg10 (say, subtitleEn) "so they take ible" -> "so they usually take ible"; tipJa and seg10 subtitleJa -> ～ ことが おおいよ (objective). seg8 (say, subtitleEn) -> "Can we check it? Then it's a fact. Is it a thought or a feeling? Then it's an opinion." (A+B).

#### Block-wide notes
- **-ible "not a word, so ible" rule**: stated as absolute in L861-864 (teacherScript, tipJa, L861 seg10); softened with "usually" / ことが おおい. If other groups (review lessons L866-870 or W30) restate it, use the same wording.
- **W29 relative clauses the regex misses**: the regex only lists people/something/a book etc. Grep W29 for `\b(a|an|the) \w+ (that|which|where) [a-z]+s?\b` in vocab-definition segments ("A toy that spins", "glass that makes", "an animal that lives", "a place where"). Contact clauses ("things you can read") are fine.
- **Heteronym read (past)**: the L863 core "I read it on a note at the harbor." may be read as "reed" by TTS after "I". It is a learning item, so I did not change it; if TTS tests show the problem, a pronunciation hint in the TTS layer is safer than changing the core.
- **Emphasis words in capitals** (TOTALLY, STRONGLY, LOVE, EXACTLY, SO, REALLY): all are real words, so TTS should say them as words; "Make X big" lines are fine except where the result is an unlucky phrase ("Make love big" in L867, fixed).
- Kept on purpose: "the chair" (W29 class meeting word), "holiday(s)" in the US sense in L867, "round and round" (L864), contact clauses, all "…" in conversation lines, the L861 JA hint, "Birimos are the cutest!", L863 "giant lake" vs "small blue lake" (the note exaggerated), story-internal "Tomorrow" / "Tonight" (L869, L870).
