I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (US praise, I say / I ask, Hi / Hello openings, ノバ, "Let's pretend I'm" starters). Casual forms (gonna, wanna ...) stay only after "Now, phonics." in the L891-895 teacherScripts; segments and the L896+ conversations use them as taught. The relative clauses (that / which / who) are the grammar of the world and are kept. #### Block-wide notes
- **-ic syllable spell-outs**: the comma-and-capitals form ("Fu, tu, RIS, tic", "Say e, LEC, tric") and the bare "before ic" lines ("Clap the beat before ic!", "The strong beat comes before ic.", "Say the part just before ic strongly.") appear across L881-900 in world-30.json. I fixed only L891-900. The pattern to fix elsewhere is `[A-Z][a-z]*, [a-z]+, [A-Z]{2,}, [a-z]+` and `before ic\b`.
- **Soft g / soft c in stressed syllables**: check any other -ic spell-out whose stressed letters do not match the sound (energetic GET = jet). Similar cases could be ma-GIC-type words or "-gic" / "-cic" endings in L881-884 if they are spelled out.
- **"that" as a word vs pronoun** in relative-clause point segments: say has no quotation marks, so "Put that in the middle" is heard as a pronoun. "the word that" is safer. This may recur in L872-875 point segments.
- Kept on purpose: "…" in the title and in "Beep…" (L899), "Beep!", story-internal "tomorrow" (L899 Ms. Emma), the Nova "she" pronoun (matches L871), "You're kinda amazing" and "gonna be great presenters" in segments (casual forms after the lesson that teaches them), "Trans means change" for transform (matches the w30Phonics.ts gloss むこうへ・かわる), "Usb" as the wrong option in the L895 quiz.
- Single flags (not changed): L891 "fast friend talk", L892 "at their own speed" (pace), L893 tank as "a big box", L896 the note naming only going to / want to as written forms.
