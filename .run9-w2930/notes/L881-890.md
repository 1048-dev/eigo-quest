I changed them to the hyphen beat form (e-LEC-tric), the same form as the app's -ic quiz choices, in say, subtitleEn and subtitleJa (27 patches). (2) **Story logic**: in L884 Tess said "so we changed the design" before the change, and "This time, it worked!" came with no second test; in L885 Mr. Shell and Tess were judges of their own invention while Mr. Shell walked in the demo. (L885, L890), and a confusing JA "かつ ひと" (L885). Every new sentence has 15 words or fewer. The check script passes with ALL OK. All changed lessons are 216-224 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.
