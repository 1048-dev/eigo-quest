#### Block-wide notes
- **Bare "tion" / "sion" in say**: L1057-1059 had them. Expect the same in L1061-1064 (pause lessons), L1066-1069 ("ology" / "ologist" alone: TTS may read "ology" oddly), L1071-1074 ("°C", "−2"), and the reviews L1075-1080. Grep say for `\b(tion|sion|ology|ologist|bio|ex|ob)\b` and spell the letters or use a real word.
- **Answers with no speaker after "X asks, ~?"**: three cases here. Grep say for `asks, [^.?!]*\? (No|Yes|Of course|Sure)` in L1061-1080.
- **"degrees" with no Celsius**: L1053 (and the conversation), L1055 ("hotter than 350 degrees") and the L1072 learning expression "about 2 to 4 degrees" never say Celsius. A US child reads degrees as Fahrenheit (2-4 °F would be frozen). I did not change it here, because L1072 owns the units lesson and its expression. Consider "degrees Celsius" at the first mention in each lesson (L1053, L1055, L1072-1074), and keep the bare form after that.
- **"reaches well"**: L1071 seg10 "Two hundred meters, where sunlight reaches well." is fine grammar (no object), but it may want "bright sunlight" to match the new L1053 wording.
