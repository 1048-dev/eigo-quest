#### Block-wide notes
- **Speaker changes inside one say**: the most common issue in this group. A segment that quotes two or three characters with only the first one named makes the answer sound like the asker. Check L1051-1070 for "X asks, Q? A." and "Q? A." patterns (grep say for `\? [A-Z][^?]*\.$` after "asks,") and add "Y says," with JA names.
- **"pause here" inside model sentences** (L1061-1080, e.g. "The main point is, pause here, that ..."): the voice reads the words "pause here", and a child copying after "Your turn!" may repeat them. This is house style in the whole block, so I kept it. A block-wide fix could say "Pause after X." first and then give the clean sentence once. Single owner decision.
- **Stress tips naming letter groups**: "on OL" (L1075, L1079), "just before tion" (L1078, and L1058 seg10 "strong just before tion"). Use the real stressed syllable (COL / NOL) or "the ending". The tipJa uses of "OL を つよく" are written, not spoken, so I kept them.
- **JA ミズ・エマ**: only L1072 and L1074 had it in W36. Both are fixed.
- Kept on purpose: Mr. Oswin's slow speech, "very, very slowly" narration, Birimo's "very, very, very cute" bad draft, "Bravo, researchers!", "Thumbs-up", "Let's find out!", the teacherScript Dr. Vale vs conversation Ms. Emma opening question time (L1071), Lumi vs Ms. Emma asking "What did you learn here?"
