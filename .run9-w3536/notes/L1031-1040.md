### Run 9 review notes: L1031-1040 (world 35, Stage 4)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (US praise, I say / I ask, Hi / Hello openings, "Let's pretend I'm" starters). There are 62 patches, and every lesson has at least one. There are no learning-item changes: school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en and phonics words are untouched, so no test or src line is affected (one expression **ja** changed in L1040: 電話 -> スマホ). The grammar order is clean: "have the right to" starts in L1031, "responsible for" in L1032 (L1031 has only "responsibility / responsibilities"), "suggest that" in L1036 and "compromise" in L1038, and no new text adds them earlier. Themes: (1) **Bare letter chunks in the formal-word spellings that TTS misreads**: "Re, c, ei, ve" / "Re, qu, est" (L1036, L1040), "A, s-s, ist" and "Pro, vide" (L1037), "Re, qu, ire" and "Re, pair" (L1038), "Bi, c, y, c, le" (L1039). They are now spelled as letters (as the teacherScripts already do), given in the hyphen beat form (re-PAIR), or carried by a rhyme (provide rhymes with ride). Bare "ity" in the teacherScript (L1035, L1040) is now "i, t, y", and "Make CU strong" (L1035) is now "Make the second beat strong", because TTS may spell CU as "see you". (2) **Story and contrast logic**: in L1039 Leo counted the votes, but the AI Leo says he missed the vote. Priya counts now. In L1034 the contrast drill asked "In the morning?" before "Saturday AFTERNOON", so two words changed. It now asks "Saturday morning?", the same as the tip. In L1035, "we cleaned the park with me" was left by the I-swap. (3) **JA**: "ふくの" read as 服 (L1033), opaque ちょうしゅう (L1035), スカーフ vs マフラー for Mayor Hart's red scarf (L1036 vs L1040/L1050), and 電話 for a phone map (L1040). (4) **Small US/natural fixes**: tap your card (L1036), a small piece of paper and think of an idea (L1037), wants to play soccer / to draw (L1038), "I pick this one!" (L1039), and podium (L1040). The facts and arithmetic are right: the 1989 list of children's rights (UN CRC), 12 + 5 + 3 = 20 votes, twelve out of twenty is more than half, the tally with a line across for five, i before e after c (receive), bi = two and cycle = wheel, and ph = f.
