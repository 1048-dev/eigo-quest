### Run 9 review notes: L501-510 (world 17, Stage 2)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, testPoints, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide). I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (double end marks, quiz "A / B" subtitles, OK, US praise, "I say", lowercase after "Now, / Then, / Next, / First,", L510 "Have fun!", L510 "speed challenge"). There are 44 patches in 10 lessons. Every lesson has at least one change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no title changes. The English in this world is warm and natural (the star story, the Happy Cat, Luka, the time capsule), so most fixes are small. Themes: (1) the "Here's the point" grammar segments read the rule as comma fragments ("We live on, for our home.", "What are you going to, for any plan.", "A plan you already decided, going to.") in L503, L506, L508 and L509. I gave each one a verb ("Use ~ for ~."). The same problem, a stray comma in "Why don't we, is", was in L507. (2) In the retold dialogue, a line had no speaker, so it sounded like the wrong character (L506 "Wow, you got a line!", L507 "Of course!"). (3) Wording a teacher would not use ("a paper full of dots", "Riko, please start.", "Show your plans and dreams", "passes the talk", "Are you, so answer"). (4) Unnatural Japanese (うみや もりや どうぶつが あります/います, ほんの すこしの ぎょう). (5) "Yesterday" in L502, changed to "Last time" as in W13-16. Every new English sentence has 12 words or fewer. The check script passes: ALL OK. The touched lessons are 215-227 s long with 97-99% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L501 Night Sky Story
- A: The bedtime-style star story is lovely ("Come up and sleep with us!", "Sweet dreams!"). In seg20, "a long ago story" needs a hyphen because it comes before a noun. "Many, many years before you and me" is casual but fine.
- B: The past-form point (see/saw, call/called) and the present/past quiz fit the lesson. The JA すぎた かたち follows the central decision. The aiTask starter is Ms. Emma, but seg20 says "Next, talk with Haru!" (see block-wide notes; not changed).
- C: Reads softly and well. "North. Storm. Orbit. Short." is easy to say.
- Decision: seg20 say/subtitleEn "a long ago story" -> "a long-ago story" (objective, hyphen).

#### L502 My Own Constellation
- A: Making and naming constellations is natural, and "Same stars, different pictures!" is a nice line. seg0 "Grab a paper full of dots" is wrong, because "a paper" means a newspaper or an essay. The teacherScript starts with "Yesterday", which may not be true.
- B: The look like / looks like point and the "It look a guitar" distractor work. seg1 "give them a name. Let's call it ..." switches from them to it (single flag, kept). The seg19 answer slide shows Riko's "I think they look like a guitar." It is valid, so it stays.
- C: Reads well. "More. Shore. Before. Explore." is easy to say.
- Decision: teacherScript "Yesterday" -> "Last time" (A+B, same as W13-16). seg0 "a paper full of dots" -> "a page full of dots" (objective).

#### L503 A Message to Space
- A: The space message and the privacy rule (no real name or address, sign "From the children of Earth") are good. seg8 "We live on, for our home. We like, for fun things." is not how a teacher explains this.
- B: The JA うみや もりや たくさんの どうぶつが あります (expressions[1], seg15) is wrong for animals. The conversation version, ... どうぶつが います (lines[3], seg6), is wrong for うみ and もり. Same flag on seg8.
- C: The seg8 comma fragments trip the reader. "The rocket goes roar! All aboard!" is fun to say.
- Decision: expressions[1].ja, seg15 subtitleJa, conversation.lines[3].ja and seg6 subtitleJa -> うみや もりが あって、どうぶつも たくさん います。 (objective, JA). seg8 -> "Use We live on for our home. Use We like for fun things. Use We have for things on Earth." (A+C).

#### L505 Future Fair
- A: The fair, the booth and Riko's speech are realistic. "Riko, please start." from the teacher sounds stiff and cold. "Riko, would you like to start?" is what a teacher says. "A good speaker looks at the people" could be "looks at the audience" (single flag, kept).
- B: The style guide flags Please + verb from teacher to child. The or/ore/oar/our review is clear. The tipJa "ブロック5 の まとめ" uses an internal word (single flag, kept).
- C: Reads well. The long quiz options in seg18 are OK because they are the quoted choices.
- Decision: conversation.lines[0] en/ja, seg6 say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa and slide item -> "Riko, would you like to start?" / リコ、はじめて くれる？ (A+B).

#### L506 Going-to Bingo
- A: The bingo game is natural ("Are you feeling lucky tonight?", "Winners, all of you!"). The teacherScript "find someone who says yes" does not fit, because most boxes are What questions. seg19 "Are you, so answer, Yes, I am." is not a sentence.
- B: The same flag on the teacherScript. seg8 "What are you going to, for any plan." is unclear. "Yes, I do" is a good distractor.
- C: seg7 "Haru jumps. Yes! Bingo! Wow, you got a line!" sounds like Haru praising himself, because Riko's line has no speaker. seg8 and seg19 stack commas.
- Decision: teacherScript -> "Ask your friends and mark each box." (A+B). seg7 add "Riko says," (EN) and リコちゃん (JA) (B+C). seg8 -> "Use What are you going to for any plan. Use Are you going to for yes or no." (A+C). seg19 -> "The question starts with Are you, so say, Yes, I am." (A+C).

#### L507 Weekend Plan Talk
- A: The weekend talk is natural ("Can I come, too? I won't be late!", "It's a deal!"). seg8 "How about you? passes the talk to your friend" is not idiomatic. "Why don't we, is" has a stray comma between subject and verb.
- B: "Why don't we" as an invitation and the "Why didn't we" distractor are well chosen. "Maybe next time!" for a busy friend is good.
- C: seg7 "... I won't be late! Of course!" sounds like Riko answering herself.
- Decision: seg7 add "Haru says," (EN) and ハルくん (JA) (B+C). seg8 -> "How about you? gives your friend a turn to talk. Why don't we is a friendly way to invite." (objective + A+C), JA はなす ばんを わたす.

#### L508 A Note to Future Me
- A: The time capsule and the notes are warm ("Are you a picture book artist now?", "Your future is a big adventure!"). seg8 "I will, for a promise to yourself. I hope, for a wish." is the same fragment problem. seg19 "It's A! You are." does not say why.
- B: The seg2 JA ほんの すこしの ぎょう is not natural. seg19 JA says you の あとは are, but the EN only says "You are." The privacy line is good.
- C: The seg8 fragments are hard to read. "Seal the envelope. Press, press." is fine.
- Decision: seg2 subtitleJa -> ほんの 2、3ぎょう。 (objective, JA). seg8 -> "Use I will for a promise to yourself. ... Use I hope for a wish." (A+C). seg19 -> "With you, we use are." (A+B).

#### L509 Starfall Observatory Review
- A: The goodbye star party is a good ending ("I'll miss the observatory.", "But Luka will call us. He promised."). seg8 "A plan you already decided, going to." is backwards, and "decided" needs "on".
- B: going to (plan) vs will (guess, offer, promise) is clear in the teacherScript. The seg8 wording blurs it. The "I think it is going" distractor is good.
- C: The seg8 fronted fragments are hard to read aloud. "See, like the sea." works.
- Decision: seg8 -> "Use going to for a plan you already made. Use will for a guess, or something you decide right now." (A+C).

#### L510 World 17 Grand Review
