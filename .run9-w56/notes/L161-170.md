#### L161 This Is Our School

The starter "Please show me the school!" from a child sounds like an order. Kiko asking and answering her own question is intended, because seg7 points it out. "It's the gym! Yes!" puts the praise in an odd order, but that is taste.
- B: lines[0] has 9 words, which is over the Stage 1 maximum of 8 (lint). The rest of the progression is good: This is the ～ -> What's this room? -> It's the ～. EN and JA match.
- C: lines[0] is hard to phrase because of two commas and a possessive before the name. The phonics and TPR segments read well.
- Decision: lines[0].en becomes "Everyone, meet our new friend Pipo! Pipo is Birimo's cousin." (objective+A+C). There is no pronoun because Pipo's gender is never stated. The JA was split to match. starterEn becomes "Can you show me the school?" (objective+A+B).
