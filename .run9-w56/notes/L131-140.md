### Run 9 review notes: L131-140 (world 5, Stage 1)

I checked the school items, vocab, TPR, phonics, conversation and aiTask, recap, teacherScript and every teacher segment (say, subtitles, slides), plus the Japanese and how well EN and JA match. The fixes that were made for the whole block (double end marks, the "A / B" quiz subtitles, "Or b", "Brilliant!", L133 "thumbs-up") were not proposed again. There are 46 patches. All 10 lessons have at least one change. **No item IDs changed** (no changes to school.expressions, vocab, tpr or phonics words) and no slide items changed. I also checked the world-clock facts: when London is on UTC+0 and New York on UTC-5, Sydney is on daylight time (UTC+11). So "8 in Tokyo = 11 at night in London = 6 in New York = 10 in Sydney" and "12 in Tokyo = 3 at night in London" are both correct. The check script says ALL OK: lengths are 199-224 s and the English share is 60-65%.

#### L131 What Day Is It?
- A: The conversation and seg8 sound natural. In seg4, "This d is quiet!" is not how a US teacher says it. They say "silent letter" / "This d is silent!".
- B: The "silent letter" term is what kids will meet later in phonics, so teach it now. tpr[2].ja ひにちを かぞえて (dates) does not quite match counting Mon-Thu.
- C: Easy to read. "Tues-day" with claps has a good rhythm.
- Decision: seg4 say/subtitleEn "quiet" -> "silent" (A+B). tpr ja kept.

#### L133 I Like Mondays!
- A: In seg12, "Now I ask." is unnatural simple present (same as L73). In seg6, Riko's question "Do you like Mondays, Birimo?" is on the slide but not read or subtitled, so Birimo's "No, I don't." in seg7 answers nothing.
- B: The same gap breaks the Q/A model the lesson teaches. In seg5, "Fa-vo-rite" models a 3-beat katakana-like reading (US: FAY-vrit). The seg20 wrap "You answered, Yes, I do. and No, I don't." has a period followed by lowercase "and" (the L73/L74 pattern).
- C: TTS reads "rite" in "Fa-vo-rite" as "right". In seg7, Ms. Emma's "Ha ha! I like Saturdays." has no speaker change, so it sounds like Birimo.
- Decision: seg5 -> "Say it with me. Favorite!" (B+C). seg6 say/subtitleEn/subtitleJa add Riko's question (A+B). seg7 say adds "And I say," (B+C). seg12 say/subtitleEn -> "Now I'll ask you." (A+C). seg20 say/subtitleEn -> `You answered "Yes, I do." and "No, I don't."` (objective+C).
