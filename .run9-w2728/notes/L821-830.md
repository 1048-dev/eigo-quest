### Run 9 review notes: L821-830 (world 28, Stage 3)

These notes cover school expressions and grammar, vocab, TPR, phonics words and tips, conversation lines and aiTask, recap, teacherScript, and every teacher segment (say, subtitleJa, subtitleEn, slide), plus testPoints. I did not redo or undo the block-wide fixes (OK, fall, soccer, pen pal, US praise, "I say", Hi/Hello openings, "Let's pretend I'm ~" starters). There are 41 patches in 10 lessons; every lesson has at least one change. There are no learning-item ID changes and no changes to school.expressions[].en, vocab[].en, tpr[].en or phonics words. The opinion ladder is in order and passes the order rules: I think ~ because ~ (L821), In my opinion first in L822, Do you agree? first in L823, First / Second (L824), the speech (L825), then the Stage 3 reviews (L828 story and mystery, L829 experiences with since, L830 made of / used for / should). The Leo video call has its 8 lines, and the safety line about address and school name is kept. Themes: (1) **TTS and sound spellings** (the main issue): the linking-sound and weak-form segments used invented solid respellings: "uv" (L822, may be read U.V.), "fer" (L824), "Meechu / coodju / woodju / didju" (L826), "Bustop / Taycare / Gooday / Hotea" (L828), "Donchu / Thissummer" (L829), and "Aw-lover" (L829, reads as "lover", wrong vowel). The bare heteronym "Bow!" was in the L827 speed challenge. (2) **Meaning**: "Finally means a long wait" (L826); "The first part is strong" right after Or-i-GA-mi (L830; that is true only for video); "What a kind lesson!" (L827). (3) **Read-aloud rhythm**: "Small words like and, to, for, and can" (L821; and is both a list word and the joiner), the same list shape in L822, "Say, Yes, I agree, or, I see your point, but, and your idea." (L823), "Maybe, Second, ..." (L825). Every new sentence has 15 words or fewer. The check script passes with ALL OK. All lessons are 210-226 s long with 100% English, and every slide item is valid.

#### L821 I Think… Because…
- A: The seasons board and the plaza talk are natural US classroom English. "Is it the sea or the ice cream?" is a fun follow-up. seg6 drops "Is it" from Kiko's line (the slide shows the full line); fine for a narrated retell (single flag, kept). Mateo's birthday in October and Amara's rainy season are fine.
- B: I think ~ because ~ and Why do you think so? ― Because ~ are taught one step at a time, and the quiz (Because I love snow vs Yes, I do) checks the right thing. seg8 "Small words like and, to, for, and can are short and soft": the learner cannot tell the list word "and" from the joining "and".
- C: Same flag on seg8; read aloud it sounds like "for and can". The all-caps stress words (SUMMER, SEA, BEST) follow the house pattern for rhythm lessons (see block-wide notes).
- Decision: seg8 (say, subtitleEn) "Small words like and, to, for, and can are short and soft." -> "Small words are short and soft. And, to, for, can." with the JA order to match (B+C).

#### L822 In My Opinion
- A: The lunch menu card, serving lunch to classmates, itadakimasu, "Can I have seconds?" are natural and true to Japan. Aino's "lunch is free for every student" in Finland is correct. Aino says "we should have more time to eat" in the script and Haru says it in the conversation; these are two retellings (kept).
- B: "In my opinion means I think. It's a polite way to start." is a clear rule, and the first use is in L822 (tested). seg8 "Of is so small, it sounds like uv" uses an invented respelling, and the list "of, at, or, and to" has the same shape as L821.
- C: "uv" is a TTS risk (may be read U.V.). Modeling the phrase again is easier and more useful.
- Decision: seg8 (say, subtitleEn) -> "Sound time! Big words are strong. Small words are quick and soft. Of, at, or, to. We have a BOWL of RICE. Of is tiny. A BOWL of RICE." with JA to match (B+C).

#### L823 Do You Agree?

Also check L836-840 for "Aw-lover" (reads as "lover").
- **"VI-ta-min" / "Say the VI part"** (10 hits in world 28: L825 and the reviews): some TTS engines read a bare capital "VI" as the Roman numeral six. I kept it (it matches phonics.sounds "VI" and the slide "vi-ta-min"), but if the voice misreads it, change the spoken form only (for example "VIE-ta-min"), not the table key.
- **All-caps stress words** in rhythm lessons (SEA, SEE, BEST, WANT, ASK): house pattern, kept. If TTS spells any of them out, the fix is in the TTS layer, not the text.
- **List of small words with "and" inside** ("like and, to, for, and can"): fixed in L821/L822. Grep L836-840 for "like and," / "or, and".
- **"I've made this for you"** (L827 core, tested) is a little British; US speakers mostly say "I made this for you". Kept because it is the tested present-perfect item.
- **"Good day"** (L828 phonics word) is rare as a US greeting, but it is a table key used only as a sound example (kept).
