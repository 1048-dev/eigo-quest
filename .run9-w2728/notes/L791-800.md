The present perfect block is well built: for + length (L791), since + start (L792), both (L793-794), review (L795), and the story numbers agree across lessons (Granny Capy ten years in L791 and L793; Haru karate since seven in L792 and for three years in L795; Haru and Riko friends since kindergarten, for five years). should (L796), shouldn't (L797) and Why don't you/we (L798, first use, tested) come one step at a time, and the health advice stays at rest / water / sleep / tell a grown-up. #### L791 I've Lived Here for Ten Years
- A: Granny Capy's story by the fire, the photo note and the tea talk are warm, natural US English. "Good night, everyone. Sweet dreams at the Spring Inn!" closes a story set at night at the inn (story-internal, kept).
- B: "I've lived here means from then until now. For ten years tells how long" is the right rule. seg9 "Fer." is an invented respelling. seg2 starts and ends with the bare vocab word "Live." / "Live!".
- C: "Live." alone can be read /laɪv/; "Fer." may come out as "F-E-R" or an odd word. "short and soft, like fur" uses a real word with the same weak sound.
- Decision: seg2 (say, subtitleEn) -> "I live, you live. Your home is in a place. I live in a little house. I live here!" with JA to match (B+C). seg9 (say, subtitleEn) "short and soft. Fer." -> "short and soft, like fur." (B+C; the JA 「ファ」 already fits).

#### L792 Since I Was Five
- A: The rainy-day lobby, the piano and the Rainy Day Show poster are natural. "Riko, that was lovely!" is fine in US English for a song (kept). The core "What have you done for a long time?" is understandable but a native would more often say "What's something you've done for a long time?" (tested core; reported, kept).
- B: "Since tells the start" is correct and the for/since quiz is good. seg9 "Sins." is a respelling with a z sound and a meaning that is odd for kids. teacherScript "Kin, der, gar, ten." should use the hyphen beat form.
- C: seg4 "Punch, kick, and bow." ends on a bare heteronym; TTS may say "bo" (a bow and arrow). "It rhymes with prince" gives the exact /ɪns/ sound.
- Decision: seg4 (say, subtitleEn, subtitleJa) "and bow." -> "and bow to your teacher." / せんせいに おじぎ (B+C). seg9 (say, subtitleEn) "Sins." -> "It rhymes with prince." (B+C). teacherScript "Kin, der, gar, ten." -> "Kin-der-gar-ten." (B+C).
