#### L781 The Village of Springs
- A: The arrival, Granny Capy and the Spring Inn sign are natural and warm. "Please do not swim in the springs" is fine on a sign. Ms. Emma's "What a lovely village!" is normal US speech (not praise of work; kept). seg8 "Then tell how it feels" could be "say how it feels" (single flag, kept).
- B: The "The ~ here is warm" frame and "Can we ~?" are clear. The ch = /k/ tip is correct. seg9 "C H usually says ch, like cheese. But sometimes it says k!" names sounds with bare letters.
- C: Bare "ch" may be read as "see aitch" or a stray syllable; "it says k" / "Hear the k?" will be read "kay". The teacherScript "Today, c h says k." has the same problem.
- Decision: teacherScript "c h says k" -> "c h makes a k sound" (B+C). seg9 (say, subtitleEn) -> "C H usually sounds like the start of cheese. But sometimes it makes a k sound! Echo. School. Hear the k sound?" with JA cheese の はじめの おと / 「ク」の おとに なるよ (B+C).
